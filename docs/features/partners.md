# Watch partners and watching together

Watch groups, schedules, private accounts (privacy rules here are strict), watching together in Continue Watching, and watch-together chat and invites.

Read CLAUDE.md first: its rules (keys, ids, privacy, licences, focus, server load) apply here too.

- Watch partners (Companion feature `partners` + core `partners/WatchPartnersPlugin`, on by default; its switch turns the feature on or off; as a `PlaybackGuard` it stops only what a group's schedule hasn't let out): watch groups (the plugin's `Partners/PartnerHub`, `WatchGroup`): people who watch one title together (a show, a film, a playlist or a collection; kept as `seriesId`/`seriesName`, the names from when it was shows only) and all see each other's progress in it; a title is what it holds in watching order (`MediaServer.titleItems` and the plugin's `JellyfinWatchState.TitleItems`: a show's episodes, a playlist's items, a collection's in release order, shows in either as their episodes, a film on its own; `WatchPartners.countable`, `KeepUp.noun` "episode"/"title", `KeepUp.single` for a film: "Sam has seen it"), so keeping pace, the row and its choices work alike for all of them; a pair is a group of two, one title can have several groups, a person can be in many. Any member invites (`askPartner(..., groupId)`; null makes a new group with the inviter); the invitee accepts, first shown exactly who'll learn their name (`IncomingRequest.willSee`); leaving a group of two ends it. Private accounts (admin list `PrivateUserIds` in the plugin settings, or the user's own "Keep me private") are never listed (`/Nitrate/Partners/People`, and the script-sharing `/Nitrate/Users` list and name lookup) or findable; an invite to a typed name always reads "sent" and stays waiting until accepted or lapsed (14 days), declined or not, existing or not — never add anything that tells an inviter whether an account exists. Nothing is asked when Play is pressed and nothing is marked for others by itself: people mostly keep pace from separate homes. Schedules (the plugin's `Partners/GroupSchedule`, `ScheduleRules`; core `GroupSchedule`): Binge, every 2 days, every 3 days or weekly on a day, at a time of day in the server's time zone (day stored 0 = Sunday); anything up to the group's furthest-watched part stays open (catching up is never blocked) and the next opens at the first release after that part was first watched (at once when it was before the schedule was set), worked out from Jellyfin's play dates with no stored state (`ScheduleRules.Of` → the group view's `LockedFromId`/`OpensAt`/`OpensDay`, `WatchGroup.locks`/`opensLabel` "Thursday 7pm"); a hard lock refuses Play in Nitrate, a soft one ("Ask first") warns (`WatchPartnersPlugin.beforePlay`); Jellyfin's own apps aren't held to it. The inviter sets a new group's schedule (`askPartner(schedule)`, shown in the invite: accepting agrees); any change afterwards (cadence, time, lock type) is a request (`proposeSchedule`, one waiting at a time, the asker counting as yes) that takes effect on a majority of the group (both, in a pair; `ScheduleRules.Passes`/`Fails`), lapses after 7 days, and is voted on from the card's choices (`TogetherChoice.Vote`, first in the list) or Settings → Watch partners (`ScheduleChangeDialog`/`SchedulePicker` in each app). Cards say where everyone is in words, never signs: the user's word (`Standing.word`: Ahead, Behind, Between, Level; a film Seen/Not seen/Not yet; a locked next part its opening time) and each partner's (`Standing.partners`: "Kevin 2 ahead · Jane level · Kim 3 behind", furthest ahead first, three then "and N more"), TV as a pill over the card plus the line under it. What's watched together lives in Continue Watching, not a row of its own (one row to check): `JellyfinClient.togetherCards` → core `TogetherRow.cards` (`TogetherCards`: invites, the titles that are the user's move by `Turn`, those waiting on the others, and every shared title's id) is merged by `ContinueAndNext.withTogether` (combined row: invites, part way, the user's move, Next Up, waiting; a title once, by id or by show) or, with the two rows, `continueWithTogether` into Continue Watching while `nextUpWithout` leaves the shared titles out of Next Up; asked once for both rows and waited for at most `TOGETHER_WAIT_MS` (4 s) so it never holds Continue Watching back. The old row (`TogetherRow.KEY`, `home/up_next_together`, key kept) is no longer a Home row; it's still an app row a page can add (with its "Watch with someone" card, `TogetherRow.start`). In Continue Watching only shared cards answer for watching together (`TogetherRow.isShared`, the apps' `TogetherCards.shares`; rows `TogetherRow.ROW_KEYS`): their standing line shows, an invite opens its answer, holding OK/a long press offers the choices; other cards behave as anywhere. Starting something new: "Watch something with someone" on Settings → Watch partners in both apps, and in every shared card's choices (`TogetherChoice.Start`). A card's group title is `TogetherRow.titleOf` (the title itself, else the shared title holding it); OK on an invite opens its answer (`InviteAnswerDialog`, with `inviteText`), on the start card the picker (`StartTogetherDialog`: `AppContainer.togetherShows` = `MediaServer.recentlyWatched` (films and shows by their latest episode played) or `findTitles` over `TogetherRow.KINDS` from the whole library, then `WatchWithDialog`), else plays/opens as usual; holding OK (TV, `RowsPage` via `LocalTogetherCards`/`TogetherHost` around Home, the dialog ending in "Customize this row") or a long press (phone, `AppActions.withCards`, `MediaCard(onLongClick)`/`cardPress`) offers `TogetherRow.choices`: Accept/Decline, Catch up to someone (marks their episodes the user hasn't seen as watched), "<names> watched S1:E3 with me" (`watchedTogether(itemId, groupId)` for the user's latest episode, for same-room watching), Watch it together now (a SyncPlay group named for the show, its members invited, then the episode plays), invite someone, spoilers by the user's own progress (`DisplayPrefs.ownSpoilerShows`), open the show, leave (confirmed); `AppContainer.runTogether` carries them out and reloads Home (`pagesChanged`, merged into `serverChanges`). Card lines are `TogetherRow.line` (TV `groupStanding` everywhere via `LocalPartnersInfo`, phone on Continue Watching's shared cards). Spoiler hiding on shared shows goes by the furthest-behind member of any of the user's groups unless the show is in `ownSpoilerShows` (each app's `LocalPartnerProgress`, `spoilerStill`/`descriptionHidden`). Where the user stands in each shared show (`MediaServer.groupStandings` → core `KeepUp` per series; `WatchPartners.standing`: someone ahead = catch up, an episode nobody's seen = watch together, the user ahead, in step) is `AppContainer.groupStandings` (on sign-in, after `UserDataChanged`, after leaving a player via `afterWatching`); TV cards show its short line (amber when catching up) and the focused title's summary the sentence. Standings belong to the account: cleared on switching or signing out. No prompt when the app opens and no "Watch with…" on show pages; Settings → Watch partners keeps privacy, groups, outgoing invites and "Watch a show with someone". Calls in `core/jellyfin/JellyfinPartners.kt`.
- Watch-together chat (needs the Companion plugin): `MediaServer.companion`/`chatMessages`/`sendChat` (core `jellyfin/JellyfinCompanion.kt`, outside the OpenAPI contract test); core `companion/GroupChat` long-polls one group, keeps its messages and emits `incoming` from others; `AppContainer.chat` follows this device's SyncPlay group, or `chatAlongside(groupId)` for a phone chatting beside the TV without playing (allowed for any of the user's devices in the group). Apps: pop-ups over the video (`ChatToasts`), a chat panel/sheet with `QUICK_REACTIONS` (TV: one press each) and text; TV Watch together page shows the chat beside the group; phone Watch together offers "Chat" on groups the user is already in. Invites (Companion feature `together`): "Invite people" in a group (phone `InvitePeopleDialog` in `WatchTogether.kt`, TV the same in `together/WatchTogether.kt`) picks from `partnerPeople` (never private accounts) and sends `MediaServer.inviteToWatch` with what's playing; the invitee's app checks `watchInvites` every 30 s while open (`AppContainer.watchInvites`/`refreshInvites`) and asks (`WatchInvitePrompt`, both apps: Join = `joinInvite`, SyncPlay join then the group's player opens as it plays; Not now = `dismissInvite`), and phones notify in the background (`NewEpisodeNotifier.checkInvites`, every 15 minutes, Android's shortest; also watch-partner requests; each its own switch and channel, on until switched off, each invite/request once per account; taps open the app, `EXTRA_OPEN_INVITES`, whose prompt asks). No relay service: nothing is pushed.
