# Watch groups and Group Watch Sessions

Watch groups (people and the titles they watch together), their records, schedules and the Together switch, private accounts (privacy rules here are strict), Home's Watching together row, which group a playback counts for, the player's "With…" chip, Group Watch Sessions, and group chat and invites.

Words, used the same everywhere (labels only: stored ids, keys, JSON fields and Companion feature ids keep their old names, e.g. `party`, `together`, `home/up_next_together`; the UI never says "SyncPlay"): a **watch group** is people plus one or more titles they keep pace on (a show, playlist, collection or film; e.g. Severance and The Bear with Tommy), each on their own screen at their own time; a **Group Watch Session** is the group's recurring time to meet (code and JSON still say "party"); **Watch now together** starts one live synced session (SyncPlay underneath). The apps say the same: the TV menu's stop (id `MenuStops.TOGETHER` kept) and its page, the player's More page, invites ("Will invites you to watch now together"), notifications ("Group Watch Session reminders"; Android channel ids kept) and the chip ("Watching now together with…") never say "watch party".

Read CLAUDE.md first: its rules (keys, ids, privacy, licences, focus, server load) apply here too.

The model below is in the Companion (feature `groupwatch`) and core; the apps (TV, phone, iOS through mobile-ui) carry it out:

- Where a playback started travels with it: playing a card of the Watching together row passes `PlayOrigin.of(card)` (its `MediaItem.watchGroupId`, kept when a show's card resolves to its episode) to the guards (TV `PlaybackGate.play(origin)`, phone `guarded(origin)`, the phone's `checkBeforeSending` for Play on…); the guard keeps it per account and item (`WatchPartnersPlugin.beforePlay` → `takeOrigin`, an answer such as "Watch for myself" taking its place, "Not now" forgetting it through `PlaybackGuard.cancelled`), and the player's `WatchCompany.start` takes it (rather than a route argument, so it survives the recap and pre-rolls in between). Up Next within the same title carries it on (`WatchCompany` keeps it for the title and tells the plugin, `nowPlaying`; the apps' Up Next asks `AppContainer.upNextOrigin(next)` and passes it to the guards). A play from another device (Play on TV from the phone) is guarded with the card's origin on the phone, but the TV's player counts it as started elsewhere. The origin carries through the details page: opening a Watching together card's details (TV `openItem(item, group)` → `DetailsRoute(group)`; phone/iOS `AppActions.open` → `DetailsRoute(group)`, both an optional argument, null for every other way in) remembers the card's group, and Play/Resume, the season's episodes, Next up and "Play on…" from that page (and the episode pages and crumbs it opens) pass `PlayOrigin.GroupCard` for that group exactly like the card (`AppContainer.detailsOrigin` = core `PlayOrigin.fromDetails`: only for parts of the group's titles, so a trailer or something similar stays elsewhere; TV through `gate.play(origin)`, phone/iOS through `AppActions.fromGroup`, which tags those items with `watchGroupId`). The card's long-press "Open the show" still opens it as from anywhere.
- Recording: at the played threshold (`WatchCompany.left`: 90 % or the end, once per part) a playback counts for those in the room (`watchedWithGroup`), else for `WatchCompany.counting` (the user's Change, else `countingFor(origin)`) through `markForGroups` (`AppContainer.watchCompany(mark)`, Companion `groupwatch` only); nothing in a live session (the server counts those). From a group's card when it's also next in other groups (`Counting.asksAll`), it counts for the card's group at once and `CountAsk` ("Also count it for Suzy, where it's next too?": **Just this group** / **All groups where it's next**) waits in `AppContainer.countAsks` (the same question about the next part is merged) until the player closes: TV `CountingPrompt`/`CountingDialog` from the nav host (Just this group focused; Back = just this group), phone/iPad `CountingPrompt` sheet (`CountingChoices`); `answerCounting(all)` marks the other groups. Cleared on switching or signing out.
- The guided setup ("Watch something with someone": the row's start card, Settings → Watch groups, every card's choices): core `GroupSetup` (steps What → Who → Release schedule → Group Watch Session → Together, `canGoOn`, `back()`, `send` = `askPartner(titles, schedule)`); TV `GroupSetupDialog` (a side panel, focus on each page's first choice, Back as a key steps back a page and from the first closes; `TitlePicker` with the whole line toggling each title), phone/iPad `GroupSetupSheet` (full screen, `GroupSetupPages`, the back gesture steps back). Easy mode: the same steps without the explanations. Adding titles later: the choices' "Add a title to the group" (`AddTitlesDialog` on both, `AppContainer.addGroupTitles`). Invites to a new group can go to several people at once (Companion feature `invitebatch`): the setup's Who step ticks any number of people and takes user names separated by commas (`GroupSetup.people`/`typedNames`/`invitees`), sending one invite each with a shared batch id (`askPartner(batch)`); the first to accept makes the group (`WatchGroup.FromBatch`) and the others join that same group when they accept, each seeing who's in it by then (`PartnerHub.BatchGroup`); invites sent apart still make separate groups. Older servers: one person, as before (`GroupSetup.several`).
- Schedule pickers (`SchedulePicker` = `PacePicker` + `PartyTimePicker` + `TogetherSwitch`, both apps): the Together switch (the whole line toggles it, with `GroupSchedule.togetherNote`) replaces "How we watch"; under it "If someone plays ahead" (`PlayAheadPicker`, both apps: Ask first / Don't allow chips and `GroupSchedule.lockNote`; shown only while the schedule holds something back, `active`, and only when the Companion has feature `schedulelock`). The guided setup has it on its last (Together) step, under the switch.
- Lists show every title: invites (`IncomingRequest.titleNames`, `inviteText`), outgoing invites, Settings → Watch groups (each group's titles and `groupLine`: "With Tommy · Thursdays 7pm · Group Watch Session Fridays 9pm · Together on"), the choices' header.
- Spoilers: both apps' `spoilerStill`/`descriptionHidden` go by `WatchPartners.spoilerProgress(LocalPartnerProgress, LocalPartnersInfo)` (Together groups' partners only); the details page still lists everyone's progress.

- The guard's question for a Together group that hasn't watched a part, played from anywhere but its card: "Tommy & Suzy haven't watched this yet" (`WatchPartnersPlugin.unwatchedTitle`): Watch now together / We're in the same room (everyone in the group here, then it counts from the group's card) / Watch for myself / Not now.
- Watch groups (Companion feature `partners` + core `partners/WatchPartnersPlugin`, on by default; its switch turns the feature on or off): the plugin's `Partners/PartnerHub`, `WatchGroup` with `Titles` (`GroupTitle` id + name, in the order added) and core `WatchGroup.titles` (`GroupTitle`, each with what the schedule holds back in it: `lockedFromId`/`opensAtMs`/`opensDay`/`togetherFromId`; the group view's `Titles`, `TitleView`). Groups stored with one title (`SeriesId`/`SeriesName`) are read as a one-title list on load (`MoveInTitles`), and `SeriesId`/`SeriesName` are still written as the first title (older plugins and apps read them; core `WatchGroup.seriesId`/`seriesName` and the top-level lock fields are the first title's; `titleList` empty from an older server: `titles` is then that one title). A title is what it holds in watching order (`MediaServer.titleItems` and the plugin's `JellyfinWatchState.TitleItems`/`Order`: a show's episodes, a playlist's items, a collection's in release order, shows in either as their episodes, a film on its own; `WatchPartners.countable`, `KeepUp.noun` "episode"/"title", `KeepUp.single` for a film: "Sam has seen it"); standings stay per title (`KeepUp` per title id; `PartnersInfo.on(titleId)` = the user's groups holding it, `WatchGroup.holds`). A pair is a group of two, one title can be in several groups, a person can be in many. Any member invites (`askPartner(..., groupId)`; null makes a new group with the inviter for `titles`, else the one `seriesId`); an invite carries the titles (`IncomingView`/`OutgoingView.Titles`, core `IncomingRequest.titles`/`OutgoingRequest.titles`; into a group, the group's); the invitee accepts, first shown exactly who'll learn their name (`IncomingRequest.willSee`); leaving a group of two ends it. Adding and removing titles is a group change any member may make at once (`POST /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Titles`, `DELETE …/Titles/{titleId}`; core `MediaServer.addGroupTitle`/`removeGroupTitle`): chosen over a majority vote as the simpler rule, since a title holds nothing back by itself; a group keeps at least one title, and what was watched of a removed title stays in the record (adding it back carries on). Private accounts (admin list `PrivateUserIds` in the plugin settings, or the user's own "Keep me private") are never listed (`/Nitrate/Partners/People`, and the script-sharing `/Nitrate/Users` list and name lookup) or findable; an invite to a typed name always reads "sent" and stays waiting until accepted or lapsed (14 days), declined or not, existing or not — never add anything that tells an inviter whether an account exists.
- Every group keeps its own watched record per member (the plugin's `WatchGroup.Watched`, a list of `GroupWatch` member/part/when, inside the one partners row; core `WatchGroup.ownRecord` → `KeepUp.own`/`mineIn(groupId)`): Jellyfin keeps one watched state per person, so someone at episode 3 with Tommy and episode 7 with Suzy is counted per group. Group marks never change anyone's own (Jellyfin) watched state, and never another group's record; a member's solo watching never moves a group. Everything about a group reads its record: the partners' progress (`/Nitrate/Partners/Progress`, `PartnerHub.FromRecord`), what the schedule holds back per title (`ScheduleRules.Of` over the record's frontier, timed by when it was first counted), the group view's `Mine` (`Record` is always true now), the standing words (`WatchPartners.standing`), `TogetherRow.turn`, the row's cards, Group Watch Sessions and spoiler hiding. Starting it: a new group starts empty; groups stored before (`RecordKept` false) are seeded once, on first use (`SeedRecords`, outside the lock since it reads the library): a together-only group keeps the record it had, any other gets each member's own Jellyfin played parts of its titles at that moment (the furthest timed by when it was played, so a schedule carries on as before); a title the library doesn't hold yet leaves the group for a later try. Someone joining later starts with nothing; someone leaving takes their entries along. What counts: marking per group (`POST /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Mark`, and `POST /Nitrate/Partners/Mark` with several `GroupIds` at once; `{ItemIds, Watched}`, unwatched too; `PartnerHub.Mark`: the caller must be in every group and every part in one of each group's titles, else nothing changes; core `MediaServer.markForGroups`); "<names> watched S1:E3 with me" (`MarkTogether`, for the user and the rest of the group); Catch up to someone (`TogetherChoice.CatchUp.groupId` → `AppContainer.runTogether` marks it in that group only; older servers: Jellyfin's state as before); "We're in the same room" (`watchedWithGroup(groupId, itemIds, present)` → `POST /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Watched`, `PartnerHub.WatchedWith`: everyone in `present` must be in the group, else refused; the user always counts as there; counts for everyone there); and live sessions (below).
- Which group a playback counts for goes by where it started (core `PlayOrigin`: `GroupCard(groupId)` = a card of Home's Watching together row, `PlayOrigin.of(card)` by `MediaItem.watchGroupId`; `Elsewhere`; `ForMyself` from the guard's "Watch for myself"): `WatchPartners.countingFor(origin, item, info, standings)` → `Counting(groups, alsoNext)`. From a group's card it counts for that group, and when the item is also the user's immediate next part in other groups (`WatchPartners.nextIn`: the groups for which it's the first part, in order, not in the user's record there; `alsoNextIn`) `Counting.asksAll` says to offer "Just this group / All groups where it's next" (`recordFor(all)`, one `markForGroups` call). Started elsewhere it counts for no group, unless it's the immediate next part in exactly one group: `Counting.label` "Counting for Tommy · Change". "Watch for myself": none. The app records it when the playback finishes (Jellyfin's played threshold) — step B. Live synced sessions are counted by the server (`Partners/TogetherRecorder` from `PlaybackStopped` with `PlayedToCompletion`, any app; participants noted while it plays too, so someone leaving as it ends still counts → `PartnerHub.RecordLive(itemId, present, sessionId)`): for each member there, in every group the session was started for whose titles hold the item — said by the app when "Watch now together" starts it (`PUT /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Session`, `SessionFor`, kept in memory 12 hours; core `MediaServer.sessionForGroup`, called by `AppContainer.startWatchingNow`), or a Group Watch Session's own (`PartySession`). When the session can't tell, it counts in each group that everyone there (two or more) is in and for which the item is the next part for each of them by the group's record; a stranger in the session, or anyone at another place, means no group. Session ids match however they're written (`PartnerHub.SessionKey`). Outside live sessions the server counts nothing by itself. `WatchPartners.contextFor(item, info, standings, liveParticipants, with)` → `PlayContext(groups, reason)` (for the chip and the cards): the user's own choice (`WatchingWith`), else in a live session every group on the title with a member there (`LIVE`), else the one group it's next in (`NEXT`, as started elsewhere), else just the user (`NONE`). The older "group used last"/"nearest group" guesses are gone.
- The Together switch (the schedule's `Together`, stored under the older "together only" name; core `GroupSchedule.together`/`WatchGroup.together`; schedules stored before it read as off; changed by majority like the rest of the schedule) replaces "How we watch" (Separately / Together only) and the Locked/Ask first choice (`SoftLock`, kept in storage and the JSON so older apps read it, ignored and cleared on save). On: spoilers are hidden by the group's progress (`WatchPartners.spoilerProgress` keeps only Together groups' partners, for the apps' `LocalPartnerProgress` — step B wires it), and playing a part the group hasn't watched yet (the title's `TogetherFromId`, the part after the furthest anyone has in the record, worked out like `LockedFromId`; core `WatchGroup.unwatchedByGroup`/`needsTogether`) from anywhere but that group's card asks first: "Watch now together" (`TogetherSession.start`: a SyncPlay group for this device, the group's members invited, the server told it's for the group, then it plays and counts there; `RunThenPlay(playIfFailed = false)`), "Watch for myself" (plays, counts for no group), "Not now". Catching up to what someone in the group has watched with it, rewatching, playing it from the group's card, and playing while in a live session or the same room with someone from that group never ask. Off: the group only tracks progress and pace; solo plays are questioned only for parts its schedule hasn't let out. Nothing is locked unless the group chose "Don't allow" (below). Schedules (the plugin's `Partners/GroupSchedule`, `ScheduleRules`; core `GroupSchedule`): Binge, every 2 days, every 3 days or weekly on a day, at a time of day in the server's time zone (day stored 0 = Sunday), each of the group's titles paced on its own (one new part of each per release); anything up to the group's furthest-watched part of a title stays open and the next opens at the first release after that part was first counted (at once when it was counted before the schedule was set, or seeded), worked out from the record with no other stored state (`ScheduleRules.Of` → each `TitleView`'s `LockedFromId`/`OpensAt`/`OpensDay`, `WatchGroup.locks`/`opensLabelFor` "Thursday 7pm"). "If someone plays ahead" (`GroupSchedule.locked`, stored as `Locked`, never the old `SoftLock`; Ask first by default and for older groups; part of the schedule, so set by the inviter, changed by majority vote, in the label "Thursdays 7pm, together, locked" and the invite's `terms`; `WatchGroup.locked` only while the schedule is `active`). Ask first: a part not out yet says when it opens ("Opens Thursday 7pm": "Watch for myself" / "Not now"), from the group's card and from elsewhere alike, whether Together is on or off; from elsewhere not out yet is said before Together's question; "Watch for myself" (`PlayOrigin.ForMyself`, also carried by Up Next) asks nothing more of Ask-first groups. Don't allow (Nitrate's apps only), whatever the origin (card, details page, library, search, Up Next, "for myself"): a part the group's schedule hasn't let out (by the title's `LockedFromId`) is refused ("Opens Thursday 7pm", why, only "OK"), even in a live session; with Together on, a part the group hasn't watched plays only through "Watch now together" or "We're in the same room" (no "Watch for myself"), and plays at once in a live session with a member, the same room, or a session this device started for the group (`WatchPartnersPlugin.watchingNow`, from `AppContainer.startWatchingNow`, matched by `TogetherSession.sessionId`). Catching up to what the group has watched and rewatching (the user has played it; then it's asked as Ask first) stay free. With several groups on the title the strictest decides (refuse > together only > not out yet > Together's question; among equals the first group) and its question names it. The TV's guard dialog (`PlaybackGateDialog`) focuses the first choice (OK for a refusal); the phone's is `GuardDialog`. Only Nitrate's guard asks (`WatchPartnersPlugin.beforePlay(item, host, origin)` = `PlaybackGuard.beforePlay(item, host, origin: PlayOrigin?)`, `PluginRegistry.checkBeforePlay(item, origin, ask)`/`checkBeforeSending(..., origin)`; the apps pass no origin yet, read as elsewhere — step B passes the card's); the answer is kept for the playback (`WatchPartnersPlugin.originOf(host, item, given)`: "Watch for myself" → `ForMyself`, "Watch now together" → that group's card, used once). Cards' words: `PartLock.of(item, info, standings)` (on a group's card that group's schedule only, elsewhere the Together groups on the title; `PartLock.word` "Opens Thursday 7pm", "Opens later", "Watch together") in place of the user's standing, and shown spoiler-free whatever the user's spoiler settings (each app's `spoilerStill`/`descriptionHidden`). `refreshGroupStandings` also reloads the groups while any has an active schedule. The inviter sets a new group's schedule (`askPartner(schedule)`, shown in the invite with `GroupSchedule.terms`: accepting agrees); any change afterwards (Together, cadence, time, Group Watch Session time) is a request (`proposeSchedule`, one waiting at a time, the asker counting as yes) that takes effect on a majority of the group (both, in a pair; `ScheduleRules.Passes`/`Fails`), lapses after 7 days, and is voted on from the card's choices (`TogetherChoice.Vote`, first in the list), Settings → Watch groups, or the prompt the other members get at once (below) (`ScheduleChangeDialog`/`SchedulePicker` in each app; `GroupSchedule.HOW` is now "Together off"/"Together"; the label reads e.g. "Thursdays 7pm, together"). The record carries on through any change. Told, not polled: when a change is asked for, the Companion pushes `schedule-ask` to the other members' open Nitrate apps (never the asker's), and when it passes, fails or lapses, `schedule-done` with the outcome to every member (`PartnerHub.ScheduleAnnounced` → `PartyPush`, the same `Nitrate.Party` header and mechanism as the Group Watch Session news: only members' Nitrate sessions, names only as the group view shows them; lapses found on the half-minute tick, `PartnerHub.DueChanges`). Apps: `RemoteControlHub.scheduleNews` (core `ScheduleNews.decode`, told apart from party news by its kind) → app-core `ScheduleAsks` (`AppContainer.scheduleAsks`: one at a time, news about a change replaces what waited about it; `answer` votes through `runTogether` (`TogetherChoice.Vote`), reloading the groups; any news reloads them too; cleared on switching or signing out) → core `SchedulePrompt.of`: "Sam asks to change the schedule of The Bear to Thursdays 7pm, together. Agree?" (Yes / No / Later; nothing for the user's own ask) and the notes "The schedule of The Bear changed to …", "Not enough of the group agreed to Sam's change. The schedule of The Bear stays …", "The change to … lapsed after 7 days. The schedule of The Bear stays …" (OK). TV `partners/WatchParty.kt` `ScheduleChangePrompt` from the nav host (feature `partners`): `ScheduleAskDialog`, a `TvDialog` with Yes focused, Back = Later (the vote stays on the card's choices and in Settings); in the player only a quiet note at the top that never takes focus (the question waits until the player closes; an outcome note goes after 8 s); it waits while a Group Watch Session prompt shows. Phone/iPad `ScheduleChangePrompt` (a sheet, `ScheduleChangeChoices`; waits while the player is open or a party prompt shows). Android phones with the app closed: the 15-minute `checkInvites` posts each change the user hasn't answered (`ScheduleNews.askOf`: `pending` not `voted`, which the asker always is) once per change id (`s:<id>` in the same remembered set as invites), on the watch group requests channel (id `partner_requests` kept; switch "Watch group requests"), with Yes/No (`PartyActionReceiver.SCHEDULE_VOTE` → `voteSchedule`) and a tap that opens the app on that group's question (`EXTRA_OPEN_SCHEDULE` → `AppContainer.openScheduleAsk`). iOS has no background check for party asks either, so it gets the in-app prompt only.
- Home's Watching together row is a row of its own again (`TogetherRow.KEY` `home/up_next_together`, key kept; a `RowJob` in `JellyfinRows.homeJobList`; also an app row a page can add), and Continue Watching and Next Up are the user's own again (no shared cards merged in). `JellyfinClient.togetherCards` → core `TogetherRow.cards(invites, shows)` → `TogetherCards(invites, cards)`: invites first, then one `TogetherCard(item, groupId, titleId, turn)` per group and title (`TogetherRow.cardsOf`: the user's next part of that title by that group's record, else the title while waiting on the others; nothing where everyone's done; one person at episode 3 with Tommy and 8 with Suzy has a card for each, and the same episode next in two groups is two cards), by whose turn it is (`Turn`: the user's, catching up, waiting), then the start card (`TogetherRow.start`); `TogetherCards.items` is the row. Each card's item carries its group (`MediaItem.watchGroupId`, not from the server; null everywhere else), so its line (`TogetherRow.line`: where the user stands with that group), its choices (`TogetherRow.choices`: that group's only) and its origin when played (`PlayOrigin.of`) are that group's; rows keep duplicates apart by position (TV keys cards by index and id). Only this row's cards answer for watching together (`TogetherRow.ROW_KEYS`; `TogetherRow.isShared`, the apps' `TogetherCards.shares`): their standing line shows, an invite opens its answer, holding OK/a long press offers the choices. Cards say where everyone is in words, never signs: the user's word (`Standing.word`: Ahead, Behind, Between, Level; a film Seen/Not seen/Not yet; or the card's question: "Opens Thursday 7pm", "Watch together") and each partner's (`Standing.partners`: "Kevin 2 ahead · Jane level · Kim 3 behind", furthest ahead first, three then "and N more"), TV as a pill over the card plus the line under it. Starting something new: the row's start card, "Watch something with someone" on Settings → Watch groups in both apps, and every shared card's choices (`TogetherChoice.Start`). A card's group title is `TogetherRow.titleOf` (the title itself, else the shared title holding it); OK on an invite opens its answer (`InviteAnswerDialog`, with `inviteText`), on the start card the picker (`StartTogetherDialog`: `AppContainer.togetherShows` = `MediaServer.recentlyWatched` or `findTitles` over `TogetherRow.KINDS`, then `WatchWithDialog`), else plays/opens as usual; holding OK (TV, `RowsPage` and the full-art Home `FullArtPage` via `LocalTogetherCards`/`TogetherHost` around Home, the dialog ending in "Customize this row") or a long press (phone, `AppActions.withCards`, `MediaCard(onLongClick)`/`cardPress`) offers `TogetherRow.choices`, per group (each naming its group when there are several): Accept/Decline; a waiting schedule vote and the Group Watch Session first; "Mark S1:E3 watched (or unwatched) for this group" (`TogetherChoice.Mark`, the group's record only) and "Mark everything up to here watched" when earlier parts aren't in the record; Catch up to someone; "Watch now together with …" (`TogetherChoice.WatchNow`, then it plays from the group's card); "We're in the same room" (`TogetherChoice.InTheRoom`: everyone in the group here, `setRoom`, then it plays from the group's card); invite someone; "Change the schedule or Group Watch Session"; "Add a title to the group" / "Remove <title> from the group" (`AddTitle`/`RemoveTitle`, only with more than one title); spoilers by the user's own progress (shows in a Together group; `DisplayPrefs.ownSpoilerShows`); open it; Watch something with someone; leave (confirmed). The details page's "Watch group…" offers the same for every group on the title ("<names> watched S1:E3 with me" is no longer offered: the same room covers it); `AppContainer.runTogether` carries them out and reloads Home (`pagesChanged`, merged into `serverChanges`). Where the user stands in each shared title (`MediaServer.groupStandings` → core `KeepUp` per title of every group; `WatchPartners.standing`) is `AppContainer.groupStandings` (on sign-in, after `UserDataChanged`, after leaving a player via `afterWatching`); TV cards show its short line (amber when catching up) and the focused title's summary the sentence. - The player's "With…" chip (TV player controls, phone Android player's top bar, iOS player's top row; never in recap clips, test plays or downloads played offline): always says which situation a playback is in, from data already loaded (the groups, standings, this device's live session, where it started; nothing polled): "Counting for Tommy & Suzy · Change" (counts for that group: its card, the one group it's next in, or chosen), "No group · Change", "Watching now together with Tommy & Suzy" (a SyncPlay session; "· waiting for others" before anyone joins), "In the room with Tommy"; easy mode shows the words without "· Change" (`WithChip.plain`). Shown only for a title shared with a group, or while watching now together. Core: `partners/WithChip` (`WithChip.of`: `contextFor` with the choice, the party's names less the user's own, the room) and `WatchCompany` (one per player, `AppContainer.watchCompany`: `start` per item, `countFor`, `sameRoom`, `left(item, position, duration)`: a part played to the end (90 %) while someone's in the room is counted in that group's record for those there, once, by `watchedWithGroup`, then the standings reload). The panel (TV `player/WithPanel`, a side `TvDialog`: focus on each page's first choice, Back as a key goes back a page then closes it, focus back on the With… button by `ControlFocus`, the button keyed `with` so its changing label keeps its place; phone/iOS `WithSheet`, a bottom sheet): who's here; **Counts for** (each group on the title, or Just me; outside a party); **Watch now together** (`AppContainer.startWatchParty`: a SyncPlay group for this device with the group's members invited; the open player joins it) or, in one, **Invite someone to watch now** (`inviteToParty`; anyone picked who isn't in the title's group gets "Add them to the group too?", the usual group invite, `inviteToGroup` → `askPartner`); **We're in the same room** (pick who of the counted group is here; the chip becomes "In the room with …"); **Group chat** On/Off (the per-group preference); in a party, **Bring our earlier notes** (pick one of the user's groups on the title → `TimelineChat.bring`, "Stop bringing them"; who brought them is shown). Counts for steers group chat (`TimelineChat.focus`): only the counted group's notes pop up and list (the user's own, a party's and brought ones always do), and new notes go to that group only (`pinNote(groupIds)`, Companion `notegroups`, `NotePost.GroupIds`; older servers write for all the user's groups on the title); Just me writes nothing outside a party; a party follows all. Easy mode: the chip's words only (TV as a note beside the buttons, never focusable), no panel.
- Group Watch Sessions (the renamed watch party time; Companion feature `party`, and "party" in code, JSON and stored data, which keep their names; the words in core's prompts and labels say "Group Watch Session"): a group can have a recurring time, one or more weekdays at a time of day in the schedule's (server's) time zone ("Thursdays 8pm"), stored with the schedule (the plugin's `GroupSchedule.Party` = `PartyTime`; core `GroupSchedule.party`/`PartyTime`; groups stored before it have none). The inviter sets it for a new group (`askPartner(schedule)`; the invite's `GroupSchedule.terms` says it) and any change passes by majority with the rest of the schedule (`proposeSchedule`/`ScheduleRules.Passes`, 7-day lapse); a binge schedule with only a party time is kept. It holds nothing back. Shown in the schedule picker (`PartyTimePicker` under the cadence in `SchedulePicker`, both apps: None or day chips, then a time), the label ("Thursdays 7pm, Group Watch Session Fridays 9pm"; `GroupSchedule.partyLabel` "Group Watch Session Thursdays 8pm") and the invite. Each occurrence's state lives in the Companion (`PartnerHub.Party.cs`, `PartyNights.cs`: `PartnerState.Parties`, a `PartyNight` per group and set-for time, kept 3 days; `PartyRules.Tonight` = the first occurrence not yet 3 hours past its start): start time after postponements, who pushed it back, the waiting request, who can't make it, skipped, who started it and its live session. The group view carries it (`GroupView.Party` → core `WatchGroup.party` = `WatchParty`: `atMs`, `startsAtMs`, `partId`/`partLabel` "S1:E4", `delays`, `canPushBack`, `ask`, `cantMake`/`iCantMake`, `skipped`, `started`/`startedBy`/`sessionId`, `nextAtMs` and the zone's `offsetMinutes`), and `GET /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Party` (`MediaServer.watchParty`, one quick request). What plays, by the group's record: in the title the group watched most lately that still has a part to play (else the first such title), with Together on the part after the furthest anyone watched with the group, else the part after the furthest everyone has watched (`PartnerHub.WithPart`/`PartOf`). The session the starter names (`PUT …/Party/Session`) counts what it plays to the end for the group (`RecordLive`). Postponing: +15 min applies at once, once per person per night, before it starts (`POST …/Party/Later`); +30 and +60 and "Not tonight" (`…/Party/Skip`) are requests (one waiting at a time; someone else's must be answered first), the asker counting as yes, passing on a majority of the group (`ScheduleRules.Passes`), lapsing at the current start time (the time stands); a failed Not tonight is its asker's "Can't make it" (`…/Party/CantMake`; also offered on its own). A passed Not tonight skips only the party: the title's schedule and releases carry on. Votes: `…/Party/Requests/{id}/Vote`. Join (`…/Party/Join`): the first to join starts the watch party (`PartyJoin.start`: the app runs `TogetherChoice.WatchNow` ("Watch now together") for the part, i.e. a live session, the members invited, the part played through the guards, then `PUT …/Party/Session` says which session); later joiners join that session, or, while it's being set up, get the normal invite prompt. Sent, not polled: the plugin's `PartyPush` (hosted, ticks every 30 s via `PartnerHub.DueParties`) tells members' open apps over Jellyfin's own session connection (`ISessionManager.SendMessageCommand`, header `Nitrate.Party`, JSON `PartyMessage` with that member's own view; only sessions of the group's members whose client is Nitrate's, `PartyPush.Targets`): the reminder 15 minutes before (once a night), "pushed" ("Suzy pushed it back 15 min (8:15)"), "asked" ("Suzy postponed 30 minutes. Agree?" Yes/No), "skipped", "without" at start time when someone can't make it ("Suzy can't tonight. Start the Group Watch Session without them? Suzy will fall behind." Join/Not tonight; they simply fall behind) and "changed". Apps: `RemoteControlHub.partyNews` (never shown as a message) → app-core `WatchParties` (`AppContainer.parties`: `heard`, `asking`, `open`, `choose` → `PartyOutcome`; `AppContainer.startParty`, `openParty`) → core `PartyPrompt.of` ("Group Watch Session with Tommy & Suzy · 8:00 · S1:E4": Join, +15/+30/+60 min, Not tonight, Can't make it; easy mode only Join and Not tonight; nothing for what the user did themselves). TV `partners/WatchParty.kt` `WatchPartyPrompt` from the nav host: a `TvDialog` with Join focused off the player; in the player only a note at the top that never takes focus (the question waits for the player to close; news alone goes after 8 s). Phone/iPad `WatchPartyPrompt`/`WatchPartySheet` (a sheet, waiting while the player is open). Phones with the app closed set their own reminders (`NewEpisodeNotifier.scheduleParties`, called whenever the groups load or a party changes; see phone-ui.md and ios.md). Visible routes: the group's line and a "Group Watch Session" button in Settings → Watch groups (both apps), and `TogetherChoice.Party` ("Tonight's Group Watch Session: 8:15 · S1:E4 · Postpone… · Not tonight") in a title's group choices, both opening the same prompt (`WatchParties.open`). Privacy: only members see a group's party or get its news (others get NotFound); leaving takes the leaver's request and can't-make-it along; prompts, the parties and the reminders are cleared on switching or signing out.
- Group chat pinned to the timeline (Companion feature `notes`; core `companion/TimelineChat`, `NoteClock`, `Notes`): most groups watch the same title separately, so a message or reaction sent while playing is a note pinned to that film or episode at that position, and pops up over the video (`NoteToasts`: name, then the words or the emoji) when someone it went to reaches that point, however much later. One record per message, with an audience (Companion `livenotes`): every message is written once, as a note (`MediaServer.pinNote`, `TimelineChat.send`), carrying everyone who was in the conversation when it was written: outside a live session, the members then of the sender's watch groups one of whose titles holds it (the plugin's `PartnerHub.GroupsHolding`); in a live SyncPlay session (`pinNote(sessionId)`), the session's participants plus the members of those of the sender's groups the session is for (someone else from the group is there). The live session gets that same note at once (the plugin hands it to the session's chat room, `GroupNotes.Deliver`; the long poll's message carries `NoteId`/`ItemId`, read by `Notes.fromLive` with the note's own id, so loading the notes later finds the same one and it never pops up twice), and later playbacks show it like any other. Nothing is sent twice any more (older servers without `livenotes` still get the old live message plus pinned copy; an older app's second copy is taken as the first on the server, `TimelineNoteBook.SameMessage`). With "Keep watch-together chat" off on the server, a live message still reaches everyone there but is kept only for the sender's groups. Spoiler-safe: a note pops up only when continuous playback passes its point (a jump skips what it jumps over; never ahead), the chat's history lists only notes up to the furthest point reached in this playback (a resume point counts as reached), the details page's "Group notes" (More, the stored entry `moments` kept; `GroupNotesDialog` in each app) only up to the user's resume point (all once watched, `Notes.listableUpTo`); the user's own never pop up. Who may read a note (server-side, `TimelineNoteBook.View`): exactly its audience ("was there for" it = in its audience; the writer always is). Someone who leaves a group keeps the notes from their time in it but never gets anything written after they left; someone who joins later never sees what came before; a session's guest keeps what was said while they were there. Nobody outside a note's audience learns who is in it (a note shows only the reader's own groups it went to), and private accounts are never listed. Notes kept before audiences keep the older rule: the writer, and those who were in one of its groups when it was written and still share it with the writer now. "Bring our earlier notes" (the player's chip panel, in a watch party; who brought them shows there, `TimelineNote.broughtBy`/`TimelineChat.broughtBy` from the server's `By`): someone in a live session can bring their earlier notes on what it plays from one watch group they are or were in (`TimelineChat.bring(groupId)` → `MediaServer.bringNotes`, the plugin's `PUT /Nitrate/Notes/Brought/{sessionId}`; null stops); while the session lasts and they are still in it, every participant gets the notes they may read that went to that group (`broughtNotes`, `GET /Nitrate/Notes/Brought/{sessionId}?itemId`, asked with the title's notes and again when the session's chat says `Brought` moved on), popping up as playback passes them (`TimelineNote.brought`), but they are never added to anyone's readable history: after the session, a participant outside their audience can't read them (`GET /Nitrate/Notes` never lists them), they name no group, and notes by private accounts are left out for anyone who wasn't there for them. When the title's notes show it's shared with one of the user's groups, the player's chat reloads the groups once for that playback (`TimelineChat(onShared)` → `AppContainer.refreshPartners`, from the request it makes anyway; unshared titles cost nothing), so new members and renamed accounts are known for the session. Older "Mark this moment" bookmarks read as notes ("Marked this moment"); the separate Mark this moment action is gone. The player loads a title's notes once as it starts (`MediaServer.titleNotes`, one request), again only every 10 minutes while playing or when the chat opens after a minute (`TimelineChat.REFRESH_MS`/`REOPEN_MS`); nothing polls. Group chat On/Off: one switch, on the player's chat button ("Group chat: On"/"Off", TV `GroupChatPanel`, phone/iOS `GroupChatSheet`, the switch first) and in the player's settings (TV More → Settings "Group chat" line, phone/iOS More `GroupChatRow`); Off = no pop-ups and nothing sent; turned on mid-play, notes from there on pop up and "Show what I missed (N)" lists the earlier ones up to the current position. Remembered per watch group as a display preference (`DisplayPrefs.groupChat`, per user and display profile, synced like the rest; `AppContainer.setGroupChat`), default on, off in easy mode (where the players have no chat button anyway). TV: the chat panel keeps focus inside until Back closes it (▼ from the reactions goes straight to the text field, ▲ back), then focus returns to the Group chat button (`PlayerAction.key` keeps its place as the label changes).
- Watch-together chat (needs the Companion plugin): `MediaServer.companion`/`chatMessages`/`sendChat` (core `jellyfin/JellyfinCompanion.kt`, outside the OpenAPI contract test); core `companion/GroupChat` long-polls one group, keeps its messages and emits `incoming` from others; `AppContainer.chat` follows this device's SyncPlay group, or `chatAlongside(groupId)` for a phone chatting beside the TV without playing (allowed for any of the user's devices in the group). The long poll gives each person only messages they were there for (the plugin's `ChatHub` keeps each message's audience: someone joining later gets no backlog from before), and a message sent the older way (`sendChat`, the panels outside the player) is one note too, on what the session is playing (kept nowhere when nothing is). "Earlier chat" (`chatHistory`) still lists older kept chat (read-only; nothing new is written there). Apps: in the player it is part of group chat (above: live messages pop up at once through `TimelineChat`); TV Watch now together page and phone Watch now together keep the live chat panel/sheet (`ChatPanel`, `ChatSheet`) with `QUICK_REACTIONS` (TV: one press each) and text; TV Watch now together page shows the chat beside the group; phone Watch together offers "Chat" on groups the user is already in. Invites (Companion feature `together`): "Invite people" in a group (phone `InvitePeopleDialog` in `WatchTogether.kt`, TV the same in `together/WatchTogether.kt`) picks from `partnerPeople` (never private accounts) and sends `MediaServer.inviteToWatch` with what's playing; the invitee's app checks `watchInvites` every 30 s while open (`AppContainer.watchInvites`/`refreshInvites`) and asks (`WatchInvitePrompt`, both apps: Join = `joinInvite`, SyncPlay join then the group's player opens as it plays; Not now = `dismissInvite`), and phones notify in the background (`NewEpisodeNotifier.checkInvites`, every 15 minutes, Android's shortest; also watch-partner requests; each its own switch and channel, on until switched off, each invite/request once per account; taps open the app, `EXTRA_OPEN_INVITES`, whose prompt asks). No relay service: nothing is pushed.
