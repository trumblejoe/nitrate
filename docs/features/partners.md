# Watch partners and watching together

Watch groups, schedules, private accounts (privacy rules here are strict), watching together in Continue Watching, and watch-together chat and invites.

Read CLAUDE.md first: its rules (keys, ids, privacy, licences, focus, server load) apply here too.

- Watch partners (Companion feature `partners` + core `partners/WatchPartnersPlugin`, on by default; its switch turns the feature on or off; as a `PlaybackGuard` it stops only what a group's schedule hasn't let out): watch groups (the plugin's `Partners/PartnerHub`, `WatchGroup`): people who watch one title together (a show, a film, a playlist or a collection; kept as `seriesId`/`seriesName`, the names from when it was shows only) and all see each other's progress in it; a title is what it holds in watching order (`MediaServer.titleItems` and the plugin's `JellyfinWatchState.TitleItems`: a show's episodes, a playlist's items, a collection's in release order, shows in either as their episodes, a film on its own; `WatchPartners.countable`, `KeepUp.noun` "episode"/"title", `KeepUp.single` for a film: "Sam has seen it"), so keeping pace, the row and its choices work alike for all of them; a pair is a group of two, one title can have several groups, a person can be in many. Any member invites (`askPartner(..., groupId)`; null makes a new group with the inviter); the invitee accepts, first shown exactly who'll learn their name (`IncomingRequest.willSee`); leaving a group of two ends it. Private accounts (admin list `PrivateUserIds` in the plugin settings, or the user's own "Keep me private") are never listed (`/Nitrate/Partners/People`, and the script-sharing `/Nitrate/Users` list and name lookup) or findable; an invite to a typed name always reads "sent" and stays waiting until accepted or lapsed (14 days), declined or not, existing or not — never add anything that tells an inviter whether an account exists. Nothing is asked when Play is pressed and nothing is marked for others by itself: people mostly keep pace from separate homes. Schedules (the plugin's `Partners/GroupSchedule`, `ScheduleRules`; core `GroupSchedule`): Binge, every 2 days, every 3 days or weekly on a day, at a time of day in the server's time zone (day stored 0 = Sunday); anything up to the group's furthest-watched part stays open (catching up is never blocked) and the next opens at the first release after that part was first watched (at once when it was before the schedule was set), worked out from Jellyfin's play dates with no stored state (a together-only group: from its own record, below) (`ScheduleRules.Of` → the group view's `LockedFromId`/`OpensAt`/`OpensDay`, `WatchGroup.locks`/`opensLabel` "Thursday 7pm"); a hard lock refuses Play in Nitrate, a soft one ("Ask first") warns (`WatchPartnersPlugin.beforePlay`); Jellyfin's own apps aren't held to it. "How we watch" is part of the schedule (`GroupSchedule.together`, the plugin's `Together`, stored with it; schedules stored before it read as false): Separately, at the group's pace (the default: everything above) or Together only (works with Binge too: a binge-but-together schedule is kept, `GroupSchedule.active`/`Active` = rationed or together only). With Together only, a part nobody in the group has seen yet (the group view's `TogetherFromId`, the part after the group's furthest-watched one, worked out like `LockedFromId`; core `WatchGroup.needsTogether`) plays in Nitrate only while this device is in a watch-together (SyncPlay) group with at least one other member of that group (`PluginHost.together`, a platform-neutral `TogetherSession` over `AppContainer.syncPlay`: its participants' names against the group's members); catching up to the furthest member and rewatching stay free, and not out yet wins over together only (being together never lets a part out early). Otherwise the guard asks "Watch together": "Watch it together now" (`TogetherSession.start` = the card's Watch it together now: a SyncPlay group for this device, the group's members invited, then it plays; `GuardAction.RunThenPlay(playIfFailed = false)`, so nothing plays alone if the session can't start), "Watch anyway" with a soft lock only, "Not now". Only the apps' guard enforces it (TV `PlaybackGate`, the phone and iPad's `guarded`, both players' Up Next/`onPlayNext` through them); the server stops nothing. Whatever a schedule locks for the user now (`PartLock.of`: not out yet, or together only and unseen by the group) is shown spoiler-free whatever their spoiler settings (each app's `spoilerStill`/`descriptionHidden`, from `LocalPartnersInfo` + `LocalGroupStandings`; the phone's cards blur by `spoilerStill` too, and TV captions use `descriptionHidden`), and its card's word is the lock's (`PartLock.word`: "Opens Thursday 7pm", "Opens later", "Watch together") in place of the user's standing. `refreshGroupStandings` also reloads the groups while any has an active schedule, so locks move as the group watches. The inviter sets a new group's schedule (`askPartner(schedule)`, shown in the invite with `GroupSchedule.terms`: accepting agrees); any change afterwards (how we watch, cadence, time, lock type) is a request (`proposeSchedule`, one waiting at a time, the asker counting as yes) that takes effect on a majority of the group (both, in a pair; `ScheduleRules.Passes`/`Fails`), lapses after 7 days, and is voted on from the card's choices (`TogetherChoice.Vote`, first in the list) or Settings → Watch partners (`ScheduleChangeDialog`/`SchedulePicker` in each app: "How we watch" chips first (`GroupSchedule.HOW`), then cadence, day and time, then Locked/Ask first whenever the schedule holds anything back; the label reads e.g. "Thursdays 7pm, together only, ask first"). Cards say where everyone is in words, never signs: the user's word (`Standing.word`: Ahead, Behind, Between, Level; a film Seen/Not seen/Not yet; a locked next part its lock: "Opens Thursday 7pm" or "Watch together") and each partner's (`Standing.partners`: "Kevin 2 ahead · Jane level · Kim 3 behind", furthest ahead first, three then "and N more"), TV as a pill over the card plus the line under it. What's watched together lives in Continue Watching, not a row of its own (one row to check): `JellyfinClient.togetherCards` → core `TogetherRow.cards` (`TogetherCards`: invites, the titles that are the user's move by `Turn`, those waiting on the others, and every shared title's id) is merged by `ContinueAndNext.withTogether` (combined row: invites, part way, the user's move, Next Up, waiting; a title once, by id or by show) or, with the two rows, `continueWithTogether` into Continue Watching while `nextUpWithout` leaves the shared titles out of Next Up; asked once for both rows and waited for at most `TOGETHER_WAIT_MS` (4 s) so it never holds Continue Watching back. The old row (`TogetherRow.KEY`, `home/up_next_together`, key kept) is no longer a Home row; it's still an app row a page can add (with its "Watch with someone" card, `TogetherRow.start`). In Continue Watching only shared cards answer for watching together (`TogetherRow.isShared`, the apps' `TogetherCards.shares`; rows `TogetherRow.ROW_KEYS`): their standing line shows, an invite opens its answer, holding OK/a long press offers the choices; other cards behave as anywhere. Starting something new: "Watch something with someone" on Settings → Watch partners in both apps, and in every shared card's choices (`TogetherChoice.Start`). A card's group title is `TogetherRow.titleOf` (the title itself, else the shared title holding it); OK on an invite opens its answer (`InviteAnswerDialog`, with `inviteText`), on the start card the picker (`StartTogetherDialog`: `AppContainer.togetherShows` = `MediaServer.recentlyWatched` (films and shows by their latest episode played) or `findTitles` over `TogetherRow.KINDS` from the whole library, then `WatchWithDialog`), else plays/opens as usual; holding OK (TV, `RowsPage` via `LocalTogetherCards`/`TogetherHost` around Home, the dialog ending in "Customize this row") or a long press (phone, `AppActions.withCards`, `MediaCard(onLongClick)`/`cardPress`) offers `TogetherRow.choices`: Accept/Decline, Catch up to someone (marks their episodes the user hasn't seen as watched), "<names> watched S1:E3 with me" (`watchedTogether(itemId, groupId)` for the user's latest episode, for same-room watching), Watch it together now (a SyncPlay group named for the show, its members invited, then the episode plays), invite someone, spoilers by the user's own progress (`DisplayPrefs.ownSpoilerShows`), open the show, leave (confirmed); `AppContainer.runTogether` carries them out and reloads Home (`pagesChanged`, merged into `serverChanges`). Card lines are `TogetherRow.line` (TV `groupStanding` everywhere via `LocalPartnersInfo`, phone on Continue Watching's shared cards). Spoiler hiding on shared shows goes by the furthest-behind member of any of the user's groups unless the show is in `ownSpoilerShows` (each app's `LocalPartnerProgress`, `spoilerStill`/`descriptionHidden`). Where the user stands in each shared show (`MediaServer.groupStandings` → core `KeepUp` per series; `WatchPartners.standing`: someone ahead = catch up, an episode nobody's seen = watch together, the user ahead, in step) is `AppContainer.groupStandings` (on sign-in, after `UserDataChanged`, after leaving a player via `afterWatching`); TV cards show its short line (amber when catching up) and the focused title's summary the sentence. Standings belong to the account: cleared on switching or signing out. No prompt when the app opens and no "Watch with…" on show pages; Settings → Watch partners keeps privacy, groups, outgoing invites and "Watch a show with someone". Calls in `core/jellyfin/JellyfinPartners.kt`.
- Together-only groups keep their own watched record (Companion feature `grouprecord`): Jellyfin keeps one watched state per person, so someone at episode 3 with Tommy (together only) and episode 7 with Suzy would look 4 ahead to Tommy's group. A Separately group still goes by Jellyfin's watched state; a Together-only group goes by what each member watched *with this group* (the plugin's `WatchGroup.Watched`, a list of `GroupWatch` member/part/when, inside the one partners row; the stored keys are unchanged, the field is new). A part counts for it when: played to the end (Jellyfin's own played threshold, `PlaybackStopEventArgs.PlayedToCompletion`, any app) in a live SyncPlay session with at least two of the group's members there, for each member who was there, the others simply falling behind with no prompt (`Partners/TogetherRecorder` → `PartnerHub.RecordLive`; participants are also noted while it plays, so someone leaving the session as it ends still counts); played to the end anywhere by a member catching up on what someone else in the group already watched with it (`RecordCatchUp`; going further alone never counts); "<names> watched S1:E3 with me" (`MarkTogether`, for the user and the others); Catch up to someone (`TogetherChoice.CatchUp.groupId`, `AppContainer.runTogether` also counts it in the record); and "We're in the same room" (server and core only so far, the UI comes later: `MediaServer.watchedWithGroup(groupId, itemIds, present)` → `POST /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Watched`, `PartnerHub.WatchedWith`: everyone in `present` must be in the group (else refused), the user always counts as there; with someone else there it counts for everyone there and marks it watched in Jellyfin for the others there; alone only as catching up). Everything that reads a together-only group's progress uses the record: the partners' progress (`/Nitrate/Partners/Progress`, `PartnerHub.FromRecord`), the group view's `TogetherFromId`, `LockedFromId`/`OpensAt` (`ScheduleRules.Of` over the record's frontier, timed by when it was first counted), and the user's own place (`GroupView.Record`/`Mine` → core `WatchGroup.ownRecord` → `KeepUp.own`/`mineIn(groupId)`), so the standing words (`WatchPartners.standing`, each partner against the user's place in their group), `TogetherRow.turn`, the Continue Watching cards (`TogetherRow.cardsOf`: one card per place the user has in a title, e.g. episode 4 with Tommy and 8 with Suzy) and spoiler hiding (`LocalPartnerProgress`, from the same progress) all follow it. Starting the record: a group switched from Separately to Together only (or a new together-only group) starts from the parts every current member has watched by Jellyfin's state at that moment, counted as watched before the schedule (so the next part is open at once); switching back to Separately drops the record (a later switch starts afresh); someone joining later starts with nothing and catches up; someone leaving takes their entries along. Older servers send no record: everything reads Jellyfin's state as before.
- Which group a playback counts with (core `WatchPartners.contextFor(item, info, standings, liveParticipants, lastUsed, with)` → `PlayContext(groups, reason, label)`, for the coming player chip): the user's own choice (`WatchingWith.Group(id)` or `WatchingWith.JustMe`, passed as `PluginRegistry.checkBeforePlay(item, ask, with)` / `checkBeforeSending(..., with)` → `PlaybackGuard.beforePlay(item, host, with)`); else in a live session every group on the title with a member there; else the group for which the part is next (a together-only group by its own record), else the furthest-along group it's ahead of, else (a rewatch) the nearest; a tie goes to the group used last on that title (kept in memory by `WatchPartnersPlugin` per account and title for this run of the app, `lastUsedOn`/`used`, set whenever the guard lets a playback through), then the first. Only the context's locks stop Play (not out yet; together only without a member there). Another group's lock is a soft warning only when watching would count for that group (a Separately group whose schedule locks the part): "This is ahead of your weekly schedule with Suzy. It counts as seen for that group." with Watch anyway / Not now; a together-only group outside the context is never asked about (its record doesn't move). Cards and spoiler hiding use the same context without a session (`PartLock.of`, `TogetherRow.line` with the context group's partners).
- Group chat pinned to the timeline (Companion feature `notes`; core `companion/TimelineChat`, `NoteClock`, `Notes`): most groups watch the same title separately, so a message or reaction sent while playing is a note pinned to that film or episode at that position, and pops up over the video (`NoteToasts`: name, then the words or the emoji) when someone it went to reaches that point, however much later. One record per message, with an audience (Companion `livenotes`): every message is written once, as a note (`MediaServer.pinNote`, `TimelineChat.send`), carrying everyone who was in the conversation when it was written: outside a live session, the members then of the sender's watch groups whose title holds it (the plugin's `PartnerHub.GroupsHolding`); in a live SyncPlay session (`pinNote(sessionId)`), the session's participants plus the members of those of the sender's groups the session is for (someone else from the group is there). The live session gets that same note at once (the plugin hands it to the session's chat room, `GroupNotes.Deliver`; the long poll's message carries `NoteId`/`ItemId`, read by `Notes.fromLive` with the note's own id, so loading the notes later finds the same one and it never pops up twice), and later playbacks show it like any other. Nothing is sent twice any more (older servers without `livenotes` still get the old live message plus pinned copy; an older app's second copy is taken as the first on the server, `TimelineNoteBook.SameMessage`). With "Keep watch-together chat" off on the server, a live message still reaches everyone there but is kept only for the sender's groups. Spoiler-safe: a note pops up only when continuous playback passes its point (a jump skips what it jumps over; never ahead), the chat's history lists only notes up to the furthest point reached in this playback (a resume point counts as reached), the details page's "Group notes" (More, the stored entry `moments` kept; `GroupNotesDialog` in each app) only up to the user's resume point (all once watched, `Notes.listableUpTo`); the user's own never pop up. Who may read a note (server-side, `TimelineNoteBook.View`): exactly its audience ("was there for" it = in its audience; the writer always is). Someone who leaves a group keeps the notes from their time in it but never gets anything written after they left; someone who joins later never sees what came before; a session's guest keeps what was said while they were there. Nobody outside a note's audience learns who is in it (a note shows only the reader's own groups it went to), and private accounts are never listed. Notes kept before audiences keep the older rule: the writer, and those who were in one of its groups when it was written and still share it with the writer now. "Bring our earlier notes" (server and core only so far; the UI comes later): someone in a live session can bring their earlier notes on what it plays from one watch group they are or were in (`TimelineChat.bring(groupId)` → `MediaServer.bringNotes`, the plugin's `PUT /Nitrate/Notes/Brought/{sessionId}`; null stops); while the session lasts and they are still in it, every participant gets the notes they may read that went to that group (`broughtNotes`, `GET /Nitrate/Notes/Brought/{sessionId}?itemId`, asked with the title's notes and again when the session's chat says `Brought` moved on), popping up as playback passes them (`TimelineNote.brought`), but they are never added to anyone's readable history: after the session, a participant outside their audience can't read them (`GET /Nitrate/Notes` never lists them), they name no group, and notes by private accounts are left out for anyone who wasn't there for them. When the title's notes show it's shared with one of the user's groups, the player's chat reloads the groups once for that playback (`TimelineChat(onShared)` → `AppContainer.refreshPartners`, from the request it makes anyway; unshared titles cost nothing), so new members and renamed accounts are known for the session. Older "Mark this moment" bookmarks read as notes ("Marked this moment"); the separate Mark this moment action is gone. The player loads a title's notes once as it starts (`MediaServer.titleNotes`, one request), again only every 10 minutes while playing or when the chat opens after a minute (`TimelineChat.REFRESH_MS`/`REOPEN_MS`); nothing polls. Group chat On/Off: one switch, on the player's chat button ("Group chat: On"/"Off", TV `GroupChatPanel`, phone/iOS `GroupChatSheet`, the switch first) and in the player's settings (TV More → Settings "Group chat" line, phone/iOS More `GroupChatRow`); Off = no pop-ups and nothing sent; turned on mid-play, notes from there on pop up and "Show what I missed (N)" lists the earlier ones up to the current position. Remembered per watch group as a display preference (`DisplayPrefs.groupChat`, per user and display profile, synced like the rest; `AppContainer.setGroupChat`), default on, off in easy mode (where the players have no chat button anyway). TV: the chat panel keeps focus inside until Back closes it (▼ from the reactions goes straight to the text field, ▲ back), then focus returns to the Group chat button (`PlayerAction.key` keeps its place as the label changes).
- Watch-together chat (needs the Companion plugin): `MediaServer.companion`/`chatMessages`/`sendChat` (core `jellyfin/JellyfinCompanion.kt`, outside the OpenAPI contract test); core `companion/GroupChat` long-polls one group, keeps its messages and emits `incoming` from others; `AppContainer.chat` follows this device's SyncPlay group, or `chatAlongside(groupId)` for a phone chatting beside the TV without playing (allowed for any of the user's devices in the group). The long poll gives each person only messages they were there for (the plugin's `ChatHub` keeps each message's audience: someone joining later gets no backlog from before), and a message sent the older way (`sendChat`, the panels outside the player) is one note too, on what the session is playing (kept nowhere when nothing is). "Earlier chat" (`chatHistory`) still lists older kept chat (read-only; nothing new is written there). Apps: in the player it is part of group chat (above: live messages pop up at once through `TimelineChat`); TV Watch together page and phone Watch together keep the live chat panel/sheet (`ChatPanel`, `ChatSheet`) with `QUICK_REACTIONS` (TV: one press each) and text; TV Watch together page shows the chat beside the group; phone Watch together offers "Chat" on groups the user is already in. Invites (Companion feature `together`): "Invite people" in a group (phone `InvitePeopleDialog` in `WatchTogether.kt`, TV the same in `together/WatchTogether.kt`) picks from `partnerPeople` (never private accounts) and sends `MediaServer.inviteToWatch` with what's playing; the invitee's app checks `watchInvites` every 30 s while open (`AppContainer.watchInvites`/`refreshInvites`) and asks (`WatchInvitePrompt`, both apps: Join = `joinInvite`, SyncPlay join then the group's player opens as it plays; Not now = `dismissInvite`), and phones notify in the background (`NewEpisodeNotifier.checkInvites`, every 15 minutes, Android's shortest; also watch-partner requests; each its own switch and channel, on until switched off, each invite/request once per account; taps open the app, `EXTRA_OPEN_INVITES`, whose prompt asks). No relay service: nothing is pushed.
