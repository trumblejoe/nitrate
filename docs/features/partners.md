# Watch groups and Group Watch Sessions

Watch groups (people and the titles they watch together), their records, schedules and the Together switch, private accounts (privacy rules here are strict), Home's Watching together row, which group a playback counts for, the player's group button and panel, the player's Chat button, Group Watch Sessions, and group chat and invites.

Words, used the same everywhere (labels only: stored ids, keys, JSON fields and Companion feature ids keep their old names, e.g. `party`, `together`, `home/up_next_together`; the UI never says "SyncPlay"): a **watch group** is people plus one or more titles they keep pace on (a show, playlist, collection or film; e.g. Severance and The Bear with Tommy), each on their own screen at their own time; the group watches each on their own time, counted by where a playback started, or at the same moment in a **Group Watch Session** (only that live, same-moment kind is called that): started at the group's session time (its recurring time; code and JSON still say "party") or any time with **Start a Group Watch Session**, joined with **Join** (the notice reads "Tommy started a Group Watch Session · Join"). **Group chat** is the messages pinned to the timeline: each group's **conversation** on a title (a thread). "Watch now together" is said nowhere any more (cards' choices, the guard's question, the player, prompts); older apps' invites read "Will invites you to watch with them" ("Watch with them?"). SyncPlay is no longer in the apps' main flow (remote.md says what's kept for compatibility); the TV menu's old Watch now together stop (id `MenuStops.TOGETHER` kept, not shown) and its page and the players' Watch now together pages are gone. Notifications ("Group Watch Session reminders"; Android channel ids kept) and the group button never say "watch party".

Read CLAUDE.md first: its rules (keys, ids, privacy, licences, focus, server load) apply here too.

The model below is in the Companion (feature `groupwatch`) and core; the apps (TV, phone, iOS through mobile-ui) carry it out:

- Where a playback started travels with it: playing a card of the Watching together row passes `PlayOrigin.of(card)` (its `MediaItem.watchGroupId`, kept when a show's card resolves to its episode) to the guards (TV `PlaybackGate.play(origin)`, phone `guarded(origin)`, the phone's `checkBeforeSending` for Play on…); the guard keeps it per account and item (`WatchPartnersPlugin.beforePlay` → `takeOrigin`, an answer such as "Watch for myself" taking its place, "Not now" forgetting it through `PlaybackGuard.cancelled`), and the player's `WatchCompany.start` takes it (rather than a route argument, so it survives the recap and pre-rolls in between). Up Next within the same title carries it on (`WatchCompany` keeps it for the title and tells the plugin, `nowPlaying`; the apps' Up Next asks `AppContainer.upNextOrigin(next)` and passes it to the guards). A play from another device (Play on TV from the phone) is guarded with the card's origin on the phone, but the TV's player counts it as started elsewhere. The origin carries through the details page: opening a Watching together card's details (TV `openItem(item, group)` → `DetailsRoute(group)`; phone/iOS `AppActions.open` → `DetailsRoute(group)`, both an optional argument, null for every other way in) remembers the card's group, and Play/Resume, the season's episodes, Next up and "Play on…" from that page (and the episode pages and crumbs it opens) pass `PlayOrigin.GroupCard` for that group exactly like the card (`AppContainer.detailsOrigin` = core `PlayOrigin.fromDetails`: only for parts of the group's titles, so a trailer or something similar stays elsewhere; TV through `gate.play(origin)`, phone/iOS through `AppActions.fromGroup`, which tags those items with `watchGroupId`). The card's long-press "Open the show" carries the card's group into the details page the same way (`TogetherChoice.OpenShow.groupId`).
- Recording: at the played threshold (`WatchCompany.left`: 90 % or the end, once per part) a playback counts for those in the room (`watchedWithGroup`), else for `WatchCompany.counting` (the user's Change, else `countingFor(origin)`) through `markForGroups` (`AppContainer.watchCompany(mark)`, Companion `groupwatch` only); nothing in a live session (the server counts those). From a group's card when it's also next in other groups (`Counting.asksAll`), it counts for the card's group at once and `CountAsk` ("Also count it for Suzy, where it's next too?": **Just this group** / **All groups where it's next**) waits in `AppContainer.countAsks` (the same question about the next part is merged) until the player closes: TV `CountingPrompt`/`CountingDialog` from the nav host (Just this group focused; Back = just this group), phone/iPad `CountingPrompt` sheet (`CountingChoices`); `answerCounting(all)` marks the other groups. Cleared on switching or signing out.
- The guided setup ("Watch something with someone": the row's start card, Settings → Watch groups, every card's choices): core `GroupSetup` (steps What → Who → Release schedule → Group Watch Session → Together, `canGoOn`, `back()`, `send` = `askPartner(titles, schedule)`); TV `GroupSetupDialog` (a side panel, focus on each page's first choice, Back as a key steps back a page and from the first closes; `TitlePicker` with the whole line toggling each title), phone/iPad `GroupSetupSheet` (full screen, `GroupSetupPages`, the back gesture steps back). Easy mode: the same steps without the explanations. Adding titles later: the choices' "Add a title to the group" (`AddTitlesDialog` on both, `AppContainer.addGroupTitles`). Invites to a new group can go to several people at once (Companion feature `invitebatch`): the setup's Who step ticks any number of people and takes user names separated by commas (`GroupSetup.people`/`typedNames`/`invitees`), sending one invite each with a shared batch id (`askPartner(batch)`); the first to accept makes the group (`WatchGroup.FromBatch`) and the others join that same group when they accept, each seeing who's in it by then (`PartnerHub.BatchGroup`); invites sent apart still make separate groups. Older servers: one person, as before (`GroupSetup.several`).
- Schedule pickers (`SchedulePicker` = `PacePicker` + `PartyTimePicker` + `TogetherSwitch`, both apps): the Together switch (the whole line toggles it, with `GroupSchedule.togetherNote`) replaces "How we watch"; under it "If someone plays ahead" (`PlayAheadPicker`, both apps: Ask first / Don't allow chips and `GroupSchedule.lockNote`; shown only while the schedule holds something back, `active`, and only when the Companion has feature `schedulelock`). The guided setup has it on its last (Together) step, under the switch.
- Lists show every title: invites (`IncomingRequest.titleNames`, `InviteSummary`), outgoing invites, Settings → Watch groups (each group's titles and `groupLine`: "With Tommy · Thursdays 7pm · Group Watch Session Fridays 9pm · Together on"), the choices' header.
- Spoilers: one rule for every screen of every app (core `partners/Spoilers`): something the user hasn't seen anywhere (not in their own history, not in any of their groups' records, `Spoilers.seen`) is a spoiler when "Hide spoilers" is on, and always on a title they watch with a Together group (`togetherTitle`, whatever the setting); on a show with Together partners, an episode the user has seen but a partner hasn't stays hidden (unless the show is in `ownSpoilerShows`); what a group's schedule locks now is hidden whatever the setting. Hidden: an episode's picture blurred and its name "Episode 5" (`Spoilers.name`; `episodeLabel` then "S2:E5"), an episode's or a film's description held back. Screens show spoiler-safe copies (`Spoilers.safe` → `MediaItem.spoiler`, never stored), made once where a page's titles come in: both apps' `spoilerSafe`/`spoilerSafeRows` (TV `partners/WatchPartners.kt`, phone `WatchPartners.kt`) in TV `RowsPage`/`FullArtPage` and the phone's `CinematicRow` (every row style), details pages' episode lists and episode headings, the player's More episodes (TV `TitleCardView`) and Up Next, playlist pages and the library list view; pictures drawn outside the cards blur with `Modifier.spoilerBlur(item)` (TV art and card-less rows, the phone's art rows and showcases). `spoilerStill`/`descriptionHidden` (both apps) go by the same rule, with `WatchPartners.spoilerProgress(LocalPartnerProgress, LocalPartnersInfo)` (Together groups' partners only). Tests: core `SpoilersTest`, TV and phone `spoilerRow*` (every row style, no unwatched episode's name on screen). The details page still lists everyone's progress.

- The guard's question for a Together group that hasn't watched a part, played from anywhere but its card: "Tommy & Suzy haven't watched this yet" (`WatchPartnersPlugin.unwatchedTitle`): Start a Group Watch Session / We're in the same room (everyone in the group here, then it counts from the group's card) / Watch for myself / Not now.
- Watch groups (Companion feature `partners` + core `partners/WatchPartnersPlugin`, on by default; its switch turns the feature on or off): the plugin's `Partners/PartnerHub`, `WatchGroup` with `Titles` (`GroupTitle` id + name, in the order added) and core `WatchGroup.titles` (`GroupTitle`, each with what the schedule holds back in it: `lockedFromId`/`opensAtMs`/`opensDay`/`togetherFromId`; the group view's `Titles`, `TitleView`). Groups stored with one title (`SeriesId`/`SeriesName`) are read as a one-title list on load (`MoveInTitles`), and `SeriesId`/`SeriesName` are still written as the first title (older plugins and apps read them; core `WatchGroup.seriesId`/`seriesName` and the top-level lock fields are the first title's; `titleList` empty from an older server: `titles` is then that one title). A title is what it holds in watching order (`MediaServer.titleItems` and the plugin's `JellyfinWatchState.TitleItems`/`Order`: a show's episodes, a playlist's items, a collection's in release order, shows in either as their episodes, a film on its own; `WatchPartners.countable`, `KeepUp.noun` "episode"/"title", `KeepUp.single` for a film: "Sam has seen it"); standings stay per title (`KeepUp` per title id; `PartnersInfo.on(titleId)` = the user's groups holding it, `WatchGroup.holds`). A pair is a group of two, one title can be in several groups, a person can be in many. Any member invites (`askPartner(..., groupId)`; null makes a new group with the inviter for `titles`, else the one `seriesId`); an invite carries the titles (`IncomingView`/`OutgoingView.Titles`, core `IncomingRequest.titles`/`OutgoingRequest.titles`; into a group, the group's); the invitee accepts, first shown exactly who'll learn their name (`IncomingRequest.willSee`); leaving a group of two ends it. Adding and removing titles is a group change any member may make at once (`POST /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Titles`, `DELETE …/Titles/{titleId}`; core `MediaServer.addGroupTitle`/`removeGroupTitle`): chosen over a majority vote as the simpler rule, since a title holds nothing back by itself; a group keeps at least one title, and what was watched of a removed title stays in the record (adding it back carries on). A playlist in a group is seen by its members: Jellyfin 12.4's single-item lookup (`GET /Items/{id}`) answers 404 for a playlist someone shared with the user, though lists and `/Playlists/{id}/Items` show it, so `JellyfinClient.getItem` then finds it by id through the list lookup (`itemById`). A playlist not shared at all is kept to its owner, so once someone accepts, the plugin shares each group playlist, read-only, with every member who couldn't see it (`PartnerHub.SharePlaylists`, run at the start of `Groups` after a member or title change and once after start, over `IWatchState.HiddenFrom`/`ShareWith`/`Unshare`, Jellyfin's `IPlaylistManager.AddUserToShares`). It remembers the shares it added (`PartnerState.PlaylistShares`) and takes back only those once no group of that member's holds the playlist; shares the owner made stay. An invite shares nothing until it's accepted. A title someone can't open on this server (a library their account lacks, or above its parental rating) is said in words, never as a 404: `JellyfinClient.getItem` throws `ItemUnavailable` (its message is the friendly text every page shows); the invite (card dialog and Watch groups' "Asking you", both apps) checks each title first (`WatchPartners.unopenable`/`unopenableNote`) and says which won't open, before accepting; once someone's in, the group's other members see it under the title on Watch groups (`TitleView.Reach`/`MemberReach` from `PartnerHub.WithReach` over `IWatchState.Reach`: none of it, or "3 of 8 parts" for a playlist or collection with some parts outside their rating; read at most every 5 minutes per title and member; core `GroupTitle.reach`/`reachNote`). Nothing about it reaches anyone outside the group, and the person isn't told about themselves there (their own invite and pages say it); the Companion never widens library access or ratings, which are the admin's. Invites are answered from the invite's card (Accept, Not now, Decline) or Watch groups' "Asking you"; the phone's invite notification opens Watch groups. The app's `partners` state follows every read of the groups (`MediaServer.onPartnersLoaded`, so Home's row and a tap on its card agree about an invite that arrived since the app opened). Private accounts (admin list `PrivateUserIds` in the plugin settings, or the user's own "Keep me private") are never listed (`/Nitrate/Partners/People`, and the script-sharing `/Nitrate/Users` list and name lookup) or findable; an invite to a typed name always reads "sent" and stays waiting until accepted or lapsed (14 days), declined or not, existing or not — never add anything that tells an inviter whether an account exists.
- Every group keeps its own watched record per member (the plugin's `WatchGroup.Watched`, a list of `GroupWatch` member/part/when, inside the one partners row; core `WatchGroup.ownRecord` → `KeepUp.own`/`mineIn(groupId)`; `KeepUp.own` is the groups holding the title (one card each) and `KeepUp.otherRecords` every other group's record for its parts, read by `mineIn` too, since the record is per part: a show's episodes in another group's playlist read that group's record on the show's page too, never the user's own Watched, which is only for older servers without records; test `WatchPartnersTest.aGroupHoldingAPlaylistOfTheShowGoesByItsRecordOnTheShow`): Jellyfin keeps one watched state per person, so someone at episode 3 with Tommy and episode 7 with Suzy is counted per group. Group marks never change anyone's own (Jellyfin) watched state, and never another group's record; a member's solo watching never moves a group. Everything about a group reads its record: the partners' progress (`/Nitrate/Partners/Progress`, `PartnerHub.FromRecord`), what the schedule holds back per title (`ScheduleRules.Of` over the record's frontier, timed by when it was first counted), the group view's `Mine` (`Record` is always true now), the standing words (`WatchPartners.standing`), `TogetherRow.turn`, the row's cards, Group Watch Sessions and spoiler hiding. Starting it: a new group starts empty; groups stored before (`RecordKept` false) are seeded once, on first use (`SeedRecords`, outside the lock since it reads the library): a together-only group keeps the record it had, any other gets each member's own Jellyfin played parts of its titles at that moment (the furthest timed by when it was played, so a schedule carries on as before); a title the library doesn't hold yet leaves the group for a later try. Someone joining later starts with nothing; someone leaving takes their entries along. What counts: marking per group (`POST /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Mark`, and `POST /Nitrate/Partners/Mark` with several `GroupIds` at once; `{ItemIds, Watched}`, unwatched too; `PartnerHub.Mark`: the caller must be in every group and every part in one of each group's titles, else nothing changes; core `MediaServer.markForGroups`); "<names> watched S1:E3 with me" (`MarkTogether`, for the user and the rest of the group); Catch up to someone (`TogetherChoice.CatchUp.groupId` → `AppContainer.runTogether` marks it in that group only; older servers: Jellyfin's state as before); "We're in the same room" (`watchedWithGroup(groupId, itemIds, present)` → `POST /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Watched`, `PartnerHub.WatchedWith`: everyone in `present` must be in the group, else refused; the user always counts as there; counts for everyone there); and live sessions (below).
- Which group a playback counts for goes by where it started (core `PlayOrigin`: `GroupCard(groupId)` = a card of Home's Watching together row, `PlayOrigin.of(card)` by `MediaItem.watchGroupId`; `Elsewhere`; `ForMyself` from the guard's "Watch for myself"): `WatchPartners.countingFor(origin, item, info, standings)` → `Counting(groups, alsoNext)`. From a group's card it counts for that group, and when the item is also the user's immediate next part in other groups (`WatchPartners.nextIn`: the groups for which it's the first part, in order, not in the user's record there; `alsoNextIn`) `Counting.asksAll` says to offer "Just this group / All groups where it's next" (`recordFor(all)`, one `markForGroups` call). Started anywhere else (Continue Watching, the library, search, downloads, a details page not entered from a group) it counts for no group, even where it's one group's next part (the older "the one group it's next in" was removed: the group you came in through decides, nothing guesses); "Watch for myself": none; the player's group panel can still choose a group. The app records it when the playback finishes (Jellyfin's played threshold) — step B. Group Watch Sessions are counted by the server (`Partners/TogetherRecorder` from `PlaybackStopped` with `PlayedToCompletion`, any app): the part counts, in the session's group, for each member who joined and played it to the end (`WatchSessions.SessionOn` → `PartnerHub.RecordLive(itemId, {member}, sessionId)`; the session registers itself with `SessionFor` as it starts); missing members fall behind. SyncPlay groups from older apps are still counted as before (participants noted while it plays too, so someone leaving as it ends still counts): for each member there, in every group the session was started for whose titles hold the item (`PUT /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Session`, `SessionFor`, kept in memory 12 hours), or a Group Watch Session night's own (`PartySession`). When the session can't tell, it counts in each group that everyone there (two or more) is in and for which the item is the next part for each of them by the group's record; a stranger in the session, or anyone at another place, means no group. Session ids match however they're written (`PartnerHub.SessionKey`). Outside live sessions the server counts nothing by itself. `WatchPartners.contextFor(item, info, standings, liveParticipants, with)` → `PlayContext(groups, reason)` (for the chip and the cards): the user's own choice (`WatchingWith`), else in a live session every group on the title with a member there (`LIVE`), else the card's group (`CARD`), else just the user (`NONE`). The older "group used last"/"nearest group" guesses are gone.
- The Together switch (the schedule's `Together`, stored under the older "together only" name; core `GroupSchedule.together`/`WatchGroup.together`; schedules stored before it read as off; changed by majority like the rest of the schedule) replaces "How we watch" (Separately / Together only) and the Locked/Ask first choice (`SoftLock`, kept in storage and the JSON so older apps read it, ignored and cleared on save). On: spoilers are hidden by the group's progress (`WatchPartners.spoilerProgress` keeps only Together groups' partners, for the apps' `LocalPartnerProgress` — step B wires it), and playing a part the group hasn't watched yet (the title's `TogetherFromId`, the part after the furthest anyone has in the record, worked out like `LockedFromId`; core `WatchGroup.unwatchedByGroup`/`needsTogether`) from anywhere but that group's card asks first: "Start a Group Watch Session" (`TogetherSession.start`: a Group Watch Session for the group, then it plays and counts there; `RunThenPlay(playIfFailed = false)`; not offered without Companion feature `sessions`, `TogetherSession.available`), "Watch for myself" (plays, counts for no group), "Not now". Catching up to what someone in the group has watched with it, rewatching, playing it from the group's card, and playing while in a live session or the same room with someone from that group never ask. Off: the group only tracks progress and pace; solo plays are questioned only for parts its schedule hasn't let out. Nothing is locked unless the group chose "Don't allow" (below). Schedules (the plugin's `Partners/GroupSchedule`, `ScheduleRules`; core `GroupSchedule`): Binge, every 2 days, every 3 days or weekly on a day, at a time of day in the server's time zone (day stored 0 = Sunday), each of the group's titles paced on its own (one new part of each per release); anything up to the group's furthest-watched part of a title stays open and the next opens at the first release after that part was first counted (at once when it was counted before the schedule was set, or seeded), worked out from the record with no other stored state (`ScheduleRules.Of` → each `TitleView`'s `LockedFromId`/`OpensAt`/`OpensDay`, `WatchGroup.locks`/`opensLabelFor` "Thursday 7pm"). "If someone plays ahead" (`GroupSchedule.locked`, stored as `Locked`, never the old `SoftLock`; Ask first by default and for older groups; part of the schedule, so set by the inviter, changed by majority vote, in the label "Thursdays 7pm, together, locked" and the invite's `terms`; `WatchGroup.locked` only while the schedule is `active`). Ask first: a part not out yet says when it opens ("Opens Thursday 7pm": "Watch for myself" / "Not now"), from the group's card and from elsewhere alike, whether Together is on or off; from elsewhere not out yet is said before Together's question; "Watch for myself" (`PlayOrigin.ForMyself`, also carried by Up Next) asks nothing more of Ask-first groups. Don't allow (Nitrate's apps only), whatever the origin (card, details page, library, search, Up Next, "for myself"): a part the group's schedule hasn't let out (by the title's `LockedFromId`) is refused ("Opens Thursday 7pm", why, only "OK"), even in a live session; with Together on, a part the group hasn't watched plays only through "Watch now together" or "We're in the same room" (no "Watch for myself"), and plays at once in a live session with a member, the same room, or a session this device started for the group (`WatchPartnersPlugin.watchingNow`, from `AppContainer.startWatchingNow`, matched by `TogetherSession.sessionId`). Catching up to what the group has watched and rewatching (the user has played it; then it's asked as Ask first) stay free. With several groups on the title the strictest decides (refuse > together only > not out yet > Together's question; among equals the first group) and its question names it. The TV's guard dialog (`PlaybackGateDialog`) focuses the first choice (OK for a refusal); the phone's is `GuardDialog`. Only Nitrate's guard asks (`WatchPartnersPlugin.beforePlay(item, host, origin)` = `PlaybackGuard.beforePlay(item, host, origin: PlayOrigin?)`, `PluginRegistry.checkBeforePlay(item, origin, ask)`/`checkBeforeSending(..., origin)`; the apps pass no origin yet, read as elsewhere — step B passes the card's); the answer is kept for the playback (`WatchPartnersPlugin.originOf(host, item, given)`: "Watch for myself" → `ForMyself`, "Start a Group Watch Session" → that group's card, used once). Cards' words: `PartLock.of(item, info, standings)` (on a group's card that group's schedule only, elsewhere every group on the title: a part any group's schedule hasn't let out, or one a Together group hasn't watched; `PartLock.word` "Opens Thursday 7pm", "Opens later", "Watch together") in place of the user's standing, and shown spoiler-free whatever the user's spoiler settings (each app's `spoilerStill`/`descriptionHidden`). `refreshGroupStandings` also reloads the groups while any has an active schedule. The inviter sets a new group's schedule (`askPartner(schedule)`, shown in the invite with `GroupSchedule.terms`: accepting agrees); any change afterwards (Together, cadence, time, Group Watch Session time) is a request (`proposeSchedule`, one waiting at a time, the asker counting as yes) that takes effect on a majority of the group (both, in a pair; `ScheduleRules.Passes`/`Fails`), lapses after 7 days, and is voted on from the card's choices (`TogetherChoice.Vote`, first in the list), Settings → Watch groups, or the prompt the other members get at once (below) (`ScheduleChangeDialog`/`SchedulePicker` in each app; `GroupSchedule.HOW` is now "Together off"/"Together"; the label reads e.g. "Thursdays 7pm, together"). The record carries on through any change. Told, not polled: when a change is asked for, the Companion pushes `schedule-ask` to the other members' open Nitrate apps (never the asker's), and when it passes, fails or lapses, `schedule-done` with the outcome to every member (`PartnerHub.ScheduleAnnounced` → `PartyPush`, the same `Nitrate.Party` header and mechanism as the Group Watch Session news: only members' Nitrate sessions, names only as the group view shows them; lapses found on the half-minute tick, `PartnerHub.DueChanges`). Apps: `RemoteControlHub.scheduleNews` (core `ScheduleNews.decode`, told apart from party news by its kind) → app-core `ScheduleAsks` (`AppContainer.scheduleAsks`: one at a time, news about a change replaces what waited about it; `answer` votes through `runTogether` (`TogetherChoice.Vote`), reloading the groups; any news reloads them too; cleared on switching or signing out) → core `SchedulePrompt.of`: "Sam asks to change the schedule of The Bear to Thursdays 7pm, together. Agree?" (Yes / No / Later; nothing for the user's own ask) and the notes "The schedule of The Bear changed to …", "Not enough of the group agreed to Sam's change. The schedule of The Bear stays …", "The change to … lapsed after 7 days. The schedule of The Bear stays …" (OK). TV `partners/WatchParty.kt` `ScheduleChangePrompt` from the nav host (feature `partners`): `ScheduleAskDialog`, a `TvDialog` with Yes focused, Back = Later (the vote stays on the card's choices and in Settings); in the player too, taking focus while it plays on; it waits while a Group Watch Session prompt shows. Phone/iPad `ScheduleChangePrompt` (a sheet, `ScheduleChangeChoices`; waits while the player is open or a party prompt shows). Android phones with the app closed: the 15-minute `checkInvites` posts each change the user hasn't answered (`ScheduleNews.askOf`: `pending` not `voted`, which the asker always is) once per change id (`s:<id>` in the same remembered set as invites), on the watch group requests channel (id `partner_requests` kept; switch "Watch group requests"), with Yes/No (`PartyActionReceiver.SCHEDULE_VOTE` → `voteSchedule`) and a tap that opens the app on that group's question (`EXTRA_OPEN_SCHEDULE` → `AppContainer.openScheduleAsk`). iOS has no background check for party asks either, so it gets the in-app prompt only.
- Home's Watching together row is a row of its own again (`TogetherRow.KEY` `home/up_next_together`, key kept; a `RowJob` in `JellyfinRows.homeJobList`; also an app row a page can add), and Continue Watching and Next Up are the user's own again (no shared cards merged in). `JellyfinClient.togetherCards` → core `TogetherRow.cards(invites, shows)` → `TogetherCards(invites, cards)`: invites first, then one `TogetherCard(item, groupId, titleId, turn)` per group and title (`TogetherRow.cardsOf`: the user's next part of that title by that group's record, else the title while waiting on the others; nothing where everyone's done; one person at episode 3 with Tommy and 8 with Suzy has a card for each, and the same episode next in two groups is two cards), by whose turn it is (`Turn`: the user's, catching up, waiting), then the start card (`TogetherRow.start`); `TogetherCards.items` is the row. Each card's item carries its group (`MediaItem.watchGroupId`, not from the server; null everywhere else), so its line (`TogetherRow.line`: where the user stands with that group), its choices (`TogetherRow.choices`: that group's only) and its origin when played (`PlayOrigin.of`) are that group's; rows keep duplicates apart by position (TV keys cards by index and id). The row shows as soon as it's loaded (kept rows at once): it's hidden only where Watch groups is switched off on the device, or once the server has answered this run without watch partners, never while that answer is still coming (TV `togetherRowOn` in `NitrateNavHost`, phone `togetherRowOn`). Only this row's cards answer for watching together (`TogetherRow.ROW_KEYS`; `TogetherRow.isShared`, the apps' `TogetherCards.shares`): their standing line shows, an invite opens its answer, holding OK/a long press offers the choices. One started from a phone's remote gathers for 15 minutes instead of one (`gatherMinutes`; docs/features/remote.md). **Join**: everyone joining within the first minute starts together; later joiners start where the group is (`SessionSync.joinAt`/`groupPosition`: the start moment plus the time since, less pauses, as its leader plays) or from the beginning. **Countdown**: the Companion sets the start moment 5 s ahead once everyone in the group is in, the starter presses Start now (TV a button in the controls, phone in the overlay), or the minute is up; every answer carries the server's clock and `SessionClock` keeps the offset (the quickest round trip wins); `SessionPlayback` holds the player paused at the start position until then, then starts it there ("Starting together in 3": TV `player/SessionOverlay.kt` `SessionCountdown`, phone/iOS/desktop `SessionOverlay` in mobile-ui `WatchTogether.kt`; "Waiting for Suzy to join" while it gathers). **Watching**: each player is its own. `SessionPlayback` reports where it is every 10 s and at once on a pause, a play after one or a jump (found by watching the player's position and pause, so players need no hooks); the others get "Sam paused · Pause too", "Sam is playing again · Play too", "Sam jumped to 24:10 · Jump too" (`SessionSync.noticeOf`; offered, never done by itself) and, more than 10 s from the leader (the starter while there, else who joined first; without one, the middle of the others), "12 s behind Sam · Catch up" / "… ahead of Sam · Go back to Sam" (`SessionSync.drift`, checked after each report, quiet for 20 s after a jump and 60 s after Not now; Catch up while paused plays on too). TV: the notice sits top right and never takes focus; its action is a button first in the controls (`SESSION_KEY`; OK brings the controls back), focus going to play/pause after. Phone: a card at the top with the action and Not now. Live chat and reactions are the timeline notes with the session's id (the chat room follows `groupSessions.current`). **End**: leaving the player (or moving on to another part) leaves the session; the last one out ends it; the starter can end it for everyone (group panel); nobody reporting for 15 minutes ends it. What it played counts for each member who joined and played it to the end (above), the others fall behind; "Start the Group Watch Session without Suzy?" at session time still asks. Extras and credit-scene skipping stay off in a session. Desktop gets all of it through mobile-ui (its player calls `rememberSessionPlayback`/`SessionOverlay`). Cleared on switching or signing out.

- A group watch stays out of the user's own history (Companion feature `groupplay`, `Partners/GroupPlays`): every playback reports to Jellyfin under the user's account, and Jellyfin marks it played, moves its resume point, play count and last-played date. So the player says when a playback is watched for groups (`WatchCompany(forGroups)` → `MediaServer.groupPlay` → `POST /Nitrate/Partners/GroupPlay` {ItemId, GroupIds, Before}): from a group's card or a page entered from a group, "Change" to a group, "We're in the same room", and a Group Watch Session (its group), each time who it's for changes; "Change" to just the user (or "Watch for myself", or a play started elsewhere) is the user's own (`GroupIds` empty: nothing put back). `Before` is the user's own state as the item was handed to the player (watched, resume point, play count, last played; Jellyfin resets some of it as a playback starts, so the app's copy from before is sent, whatever the order); when Jellyfin says the playback stopped (any app, or its session timing out), the Companion puts exactly that back (`GroupPlays.Restore`, reason `UpdateUserData`), kept 12 hours, handed over between logic versions (`groupplays.v1`). The group's side is its own record and resume point. The diary records a group watch with its groups (`DiaryEntry.GroupIds`; `DiaryRecorder` asks `GroupPlays.For`), shown "With Tommy & Suzy" (core `Diary.withLabel`), and stats keep it apart (core `stats/SplitStatsBuilder`: the user's own figures from their record and own diary entries, "With your watch groups" from group entries, with each group's share). Group watches from before this stay in the user's history (cleaned up by hand).
- Two watched states, never mixed: the user's own (Jellyfin's played marks) and each group's record. - Group resume points (Companion feature `groupresume`; `WatchGroup.Resume` member/part/position/when, at most 20 per member per group, cleared when the part is counted or marked watched for that member, taken along on leaving; `PUT /Nitrate/Partners/Groups/{id}/Resume` {ItemId, PositionMs} (0 clears), `GroupView.Resume` the caller's; core `WatchGroup.resume`, `MediaServer.setGroupResume`). - Joining a group with someone else's playlist: Jellyfin keeps a playlist to its owner, and the Companion shares it with each member as the groups are next read (`PartnerHub.SharePlaylists`). - Invite notifications: the Companion tells only the invitee's open Nitrate apps the moment an invite to an account that exists is made (`PartnerHub.RequestMade` → `PartyPush` kind `request`, nothing in it but the kind; the inviter learns nothing); the app reads its groups again and `checkInvites` notifies at once (`RemoteControlHub.inviteNews`). - Starting a Group Watch Session checks the part first (`WatchPartnersPlugin.refusedInSession`): a part the group's schedule doesn't let out yet (Don't allow) is refused before anyone is asked to join, with the reason. - Schedules (Companion `GroupSchedule` + `ScheduleRules`; core `GroupSchedule`, both apps' `PacePicker`): the pace is Binge, Daily, Every few days (`days`, `EveryDays` 2–7), Weekly (`Weekdays`, any set; empty reads the older single `Day`; `EveryWeeks` 1 or 2, counted from the week it was set) or Monthly (`MonthDay` 1–28); the older `days2`/`days3` still read as before and show as Every few days in the pickers (`pickerCadence`, `picked`). "The first part": Open now (the default) or On the first release (`FirstLocked`: nothing plays before the first release time after the schedule was set). With several titles, Separate (the default: each keeps its own pace) or Sequential (`Sequential`, `TitleOrder` set in the picker with ▲ ▼: the titles as one run in that order, the pace counting across them; `ScheduleRules.InOrder`/`Spread` put the run's lock back on each title, the ones after it waiting from their first part); the picker says a mixed order (interleaving) is for a playlist (`ORDER_NOTE`). The label reads "Mon, Wed and Fri 9pm", "Every other Thursday 7pm", "Monthly on the 1st, 8pm", "…, 2 at a time"; invites list the first part and Sequential among the schedule's points. Changes still need most of the group.
