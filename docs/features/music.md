# Music, audiobooks and Android Auto

The listening player, audiobooks, Android Auto, lyrics.

Read CLAUDE.md first: its rules (keys, ids, privacy, licences, focus, server load) apply here too.

- Listening player (music; audiobooks next): core `music/Listening` (the interface: `ListenState` queue in playing order, index, playing, shuffle, `Repeat`; play/playNext/addToQueue/toggle/next/previous (`ListenRules.RESTART_AFTER_MS`: past 3 s Previous restarts the song)/seek/skipTo/shuffle/repeat/remove/move/stop), `Loudness` (Jellyfin's `NormalizationGain` per track and album, `MediaItem.normalizationGain`, as player volume, quieting only, never below `FLOOR`; `Levelling` Off / Every song (default) / By album = `PlaybackSettings.levelling`, per device) and `ListenStream` (the file as it is when Media3 reads its container, else converted to AAC 256k). `AppContainer.listening` (`AppPlatform.listening`, `IosListening` on iOS) lives as long as the app. core-android `playback/AndroidListening`: one ExoPlayer playlist (gapless), FFmpeg audio for formats the device lacks, music audio focus and becoming-noisy pause, the sign-in added per request (`ResolvingDataSource`, so account switches never use a stale one); a song failing as it is is replaced by its converted stream at the same spot, one failing converted is skipped (`problem`); levelling on each song change (album gains fetched once); reports per song through `MediaServer.listenUrl`/`reportListen`/`reportListenStopped` (`Audio/{id}/stream` static or converted, `Sessions/Playing`, each play its own play session, every `REPORT_EVERY_MS`), a song left part way reported where it was left (`onPositionDiscontinuity`), so skips never count as played; `onPlayer` hands the player to the phone's background service when playing starts. `ListeningTest` (mobile) drives it on real WAVs. Phone: `AppActions.playItems` (album Play/Shuffle/track taps/Instant mix, playlists of songs) and a single song through `go` (its album queued around it) go to the listening player, anything else to `playQueue`; `MiniPlayer` above the nav pill on tab screens (with `PlayingElsewhereBar` above it when both show; `LocalPlayingElsewhere` makes room) opens `NowPlayingRoute` (`NowPlayingScreen`: cover, seek slider, shuffle/previous/play/next/repeat, Up next with tap to skip and remove, Lyrics); Settings → Picture & sound → "Music loudness"; `NitrateApp` gives the player to `BackgroundPlayback` (which follows whichever player last asked, video or music); a video opening pauses the music; switching accounts or signing out stops it (`leaveSession`). Screens take a `player` for tests (`testing/FakeListening`). TV: the nav host's `listen` (album/playlist Play/Shuffle/Instant mix via `onPlayQueue`; a single song through the playback gate, its album queued around it) plays songs in the listening player and opens `NowPlayingRoute` (`ui/music/NowPlayingScreen`: cover and song, progress, controls whose ◀▶ stay in their row (`focusProperties`), Up next (OK skips to it) or lyrics, media keys); Back leaves it playing; while music plays the menu shows a "Now playing" stop first (`MenuCatalog.nowPlaying`, `NOW_PLAYING_STOP`, added by `MainMenu`, never in the user's arrangement); media keys nothing on screen used work the music from any page (`MainActivity.onKeyDown`); a video pauses it; Picture & sound → "Music loudness". Music downloads (phone): `MusicDownloadButton` on album pages and playlists of songs queues every song through `DownloadManager.enqueue` at `ORIGINAL` (the file as it is; "Downloading n/N", "Downloaded"; removing asks first); the listening player plays a downloaded song from its file (core `music/ListenDownloads`, given by `AppContainer` over `downloads.videoPath`/`recordPlayback`; a local file that fails falls back to the server's converted stream) and keeps its play on the download (`played`), sent by `syncWatchState` as films are.
- Audiobooks (phone and TV, in the listening player): Jellyfin's `AudioBook` items are `ItemKind.AUDIOBOOK` (playable); rows `home/continue_listening` (resumable audiobooks) and, for Books libraries, `library/<id>/continue_listening`. core `music/Audiobooks` (`isBook`, `SPEEDS`, back 15 s / ahead 30 s, `chapterAt`/`nextChapterStart`/`previousChapterStart`/`chapterEnd`, `timeLeft`) and `Sleep` (after N minutes, end of chapter). `Listening.play(…, startMs)` resumes a book where it was left; `skipBy`, `setSpeed` (kept for books, music always 1×; in `ListenState.speed`), `setSleep` (`ListenState.sleep`/`sleepEndsAtMs`). In `AndroidListening` Next/Previous step chapters inside a book (its chapters fetched with the item when a row didn't carry them), and books aren't levelled. Play on a book anywhere (phone `go`, TV gate) starts it in the listening player and opens Now Playing, which in book mode shows −15 s/+30 s instead of shuffle/repeat, the chapter, time left, speed and sleep (phone `BookChoices` chips, TV cycling buttons) and a Chapters list instead of Up next. Bookmarks: core `music/Bookmarks` (`Bookmark` position + optional note; `add` keeps the book's order and replaces one within 5 s, `place` = "Chapter 12 · 1:23:45"), kept per book as shared display-settings entries `bookmarks:<item id>` (`DisplayProfiles.BOOKMARKS`, the same on every device, merged book by book) through `AppContainer.bookmarks` (`BookmarkStore`); Now Playing's "Bookmark" marks the place in one press, the phone lists them (tap to go there, Note, remove) and the TV lists them above the chapters (OK to go there, Remove). Books download from their details page in one tap as they are (`DownloadAction`: sound skips the quality dialog); offline a book keeps its place from 30 s and counts as read only in its last 2 minutes (`DownloadManager.recordPlayback`, `BOOK_MIN_RESUME_MS`/`BOOK_END_MS`), and Play resumes from the download's unsynced place (`resumePositionMs`).
- Music: `MediaServer.albumTracks`/`artistAlbums`/`albumArtists`/`instantMix`; tracks carry `album`/`albumId`/`artists` and fall back to the album cover. Players show the cover via `PlayerView.showCover` (Media3 only shows embedded art). Lyrics: `MediaServer.lyrics` (`Audio/{id}/Lyrics`; 404 = none; timed when every line has a start, since 12.4 often omits `IsSynced`) as core `music/Lyrics` (`currentIndex`); each app's `LyricsView` over/beside the cover from the player's "Lyrics" button (phone: tap a timed line to go there).
- Android Auto (phone app): mobile-ui `platform/PlaybackService` is a `MediaLibraryService` (manifest also declares `MediaBrowserService` and `automotive_app_desc.xml`); its session's player is the one in the background, else the listening player, wrapped in `CarPlayer`. The tree is core `car/CarLibrary` (tabs Listen now: Shuffle all music, audiobooks under way, recently added albums / Music: Recently added, Albums, Artists (per music library when several), Playlists (no video ones) / Audiobooks; ids say how to fetch: `album:`, `artist:`, `playlist:`, playable `play:<context>|<itemId>`, so a song plays on through its album or playlist and a book resumes where it was left), `search`, and voice requests (`forVoice`: `bestMatch` exact name, then a prefix, then the server's first; nothing named = shuffle all; an artist = instant mix). `CarBrowsing` (the session callback) restores the sign-in when the app wasn't opened ("Open Nitrate on your phone to sign in" otherwise) and resolves a requested id or search into a `CarQueue` held behind one stand-in item (`hold`/`take`); `CarPlayer` turns setting that item into `AndroidListening.play` (adding into `addToQueue`/`playNext`) and Next/Previous into the listening player's (chapters, restart after 3 s). Artwork reaches the car through the `CarArt` content provider (`<app id>.carart`; only the signed-in server's pictures, cached in `cache/car-art`, 300 kept). Never video.
