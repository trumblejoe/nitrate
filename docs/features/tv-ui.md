# TV pages, rows and navigation

How the TV app's pages are built: rows, full art, menu, tabs, Customize, layouts and the small TV-only extras. The TV focus rules stay in CLAUDE.md.

Read CLAUDE.md first: its rules (keys, ids, privacy, licences, focus, server load) apply here too.

- TV home and library pages both render through `ui/rows/RowsPage`; add row behaviour there, not per screen. TV Home is `FullArtPage` (one title full screen; ◀▶ items, ▲▼ rows, or with `DisplayPrefs.tvUpDownAlong` / `PageLook.upDownAlong` ("Up/Down: Along the row, then rows", off by default, in Customize's layout and Display → Layout for every page) ▲▼ step items too, going to the next row's first title or the previous row's last only past the ends) or `RowsPage`, chosen by `DisplayPrefs.tvArrangement` (core `PageArrangement`: Full art / Rows one at a time / Stacked rows, one choice; stored under the old name `tvHome`, the old separate stacked switch dropped, so stacked pages saved that way read as Rows one at a time); every top-level page (Home, libraries, custom pages) wraps itself in `MainMenu(currentStopId)`, which shows `TvMenu` from the nav host's `LocalMenuCatalog` (libraries via `rememberLibraries` over core-android `LibraryCache`, which the phone's Libraries tab also uses) in the user's `DisplayPrefs.tvMenu` placement (core `TvMenuStyle`; Settings → Display → Menu offers Overlay (default; it opens as the dial does, with Menu or Up past the page's top: no choice of arrow; the old `DisplayPrefs.tvPopupKey` stays stored but is no longer read), Side rail (with Rail side Left/Right: `LEFT`/`RIGHT`), Top bar and Dial): the overlay (`OVERLAY`, the default for new users) and the side rail show nothing while browsing (no strip, the page has the whole screen, `MenuInsets()` all round). The overlay is a centred pop-up (`MenuPopup.kt`): opening dims the page and a solid card scales up in the middle (160 ms; only the dimming fades, so the page never shows through the card; rows of tiles are centred and ◀▶ stay within a row; focus is asked for as soon as it's composed, never after the animation); its top row holds a Search tile and the avatar tile (the user's picture or initial and name, "Settings, switch user"; said "<name>, account"), which opens the same `AccountPanel` as the corner (Switch user, Settings, display profile, Sign out; choosing one closes the pop-up first, `AccountPanel(onChosen)`; Back from the panel returns to the avatar tile, the pop-up still open, and the pop-up doesn't close while the panel has focus); below, the menu's stops (`MenuCatalog.stops`, the user's order, More's stop left out) as a grid of large tiles (icon over words, never icons alone; the current page's marked with a dot), then More's stops (`MenuCatalog.more`, `TvMenu(more)`) under a "More" heading instead of a More tile (the More page stays for the other styles; a page kept in More opens the pop-up on its tile there). Focus starts on the current page's tile (a group's tile when the page is in a group); all four arrows move round the grid (2D focus search over plain rows) and nothing past its edges leaves the card (the card's `focusProperties.exit` holds only moves, so closing can hand focus back by request); a group's tile ("n inside ›") opens its stops as a small grid of their own under the group's name ("Back returns to the menu"), focus on the first, and Back steps out to the main grid with focus on the group's tile (taken in the card's `onPreviewKeyEvent` so the menu's Back doesn't close it); Customize this page is a tile (not in easy mode); choosing a tile closes the card first, then goes; Back or Menu close it, focus going back where it was on the page (`FocusReturn`); focus leaving it for good closes it. The card is sized to the screen (at most 900 dp wide, 5 columns on a 960 dp TV) and scrolls inside when there are many stops, the focused row brought into view with a margin so its grown tile isn't cut off. `tvMenuBlack` makes the card solid black. The overlay opens with the remote's Menu key or Up pressed past the page's top (as the dial): `TvMenu(opensWith)`, always Up, makes `LocalMenuSide`/`LocalMenuEdge` and the page's `focusProperties.exit` answer for that key only, and its `EdgeCatcher` ("Menu") sits on that edge (along the top for Up, where the other styles' path to the corner was); moving off the other edges does nothing special. The overlay has no profile corner (the pop-up holds Search and the avatar; `TvMenu`'s `showCorner`, no `CornerRoom` kept clear). The side rail opens over the page (`SideMenu` in `MenuBars.kt`, a plain focus-grouped `Column`, no `NavigationDrawer`): a column of icons and words (never icons alone) at its edge; it appears at once with focus on the current page's stop (asked for as soon as the column is composed, no animation to wait for), ▲▼ move, nothing past the ends (only moves are held by the column's `focusProperties.exit`, so closing can hand focus back by request), groups open inline (as below), choosing a stop closes it first, and towards the page (Right; Left for the rail on the right), Back (taken as a key too, after an open group has folded) or Menu close it, focus going back where it was on the page (`FocusReturn`); focus leaving it for good closes it; invisible slivers at the menu's edge and along the top (`EdgeCatcher`, named "Menu" / "Account and search") give moving off the page something to land on so the page's `exit` can send it to the menu or the corner; a catcher acts only when its own arrow was pressed this half second (`ArrowPress`, noted in `TvMenu`'s `onPreviewKeyEvent`; the dial's top sliver too), and focus landing there any other way goes back to the page, since a page swapped for one with nothing focusable yet (choosing a page from the menu) let focus fall on the catcher and the pop-up opened by itself (`overlayStaysShutWhenFocusLandsOnTheEdgeByItself`). Top bar: at the top on the first row and the bottom on the last, as reported through `LocalMenuRows`; dial: cardinal-only rings. `tvMenuAutoHide` ("Fade in") applies to the Top bar only now (the overlay and rail are hidden anyway; the old `FADE` style reads as Top bar + fade) and `tvMenuBlack` ("Black menu": overlay card, rail, bar and dial on solid black). `tvMenu` is always written (`@EncodeDefault`); settings saved before the overlay existed have no `tvMenu` (the left rail was the unwritten default) and `DisplayPrefs.decode` (used by `DisplaySettingsStore`) keeps them on the rail, so only new users start on the overlay. The profile corner (`ProfileCorner.kt`) sits top right on every `MainMenu` page with the rail, the Top bar and the dial (not the overlay, whose pop-up holds Search and the avatar): Search (magnifier, said "Search") and the avatar (the user's Jellyfin picture from `savedAccounts`, else their initial; said "<name>, account"); Up from the page's top (its first row or tabs; full art through `LocalMenuEdge`) reaches the avatar, Left goes to Search, Down or Back go back to the page, Left from Search (Right from the avatar for the rail on the right) opens the menu; on the Top bar Up from the bar reaches it, on the dial Up past the top spoke's last stop (or with nothing up there). The avatar opens `AccountPanel` (a `TvDialog` side panel; Back closes it and focus returns to the avatar): the user's name and server, Switch user (held OK: the quick switch), Settings, the display profile this TV uses (the TV built-in and the user's own, when they have any; `useProfile`), and Sign out (`MenuActions.onSignOut` → `MenuCatalog.signOut`; easy mode asks first). Its room is kept clear through `LocalCornerRoom` (`CornerRoom`), which `ThemedPage` adds to `LocalClockRoom` and puts the clock beside; the Top bar keeps its end clear for it. With the overlay and the rail, Search, Settings and Switch user (`MenuStops.IN_CORNER`) leave the menu's stops (`MenuStops.shownIn`, and More's), keeping their ids and their places in `tvMenuStops`; the Top bar and the dial keep them as stops too. Settings is thus always reachable, in easy mode as well, through the corner (the overlay: the pop-up's avatar tile); reached by moving off the page's edge via `LocalMenuEdge` or the Menu key, never by taking over Back; focus leaving any page towards the menu is redirected to it (`TvMenu`'s page `focusProperties { exit }` → `toMenu`), so it always lands on a stop; with Fade in the menu always lays over the page (never a blank strip of its own). Library pages are laid out like Home unless `DisplayPrefs.tvLibraryArrangement` (stored as `tvLibraryLayout`) says otherwise (full art with the tabs top right via `FullArtPage(header)`, the menu over it); switching tabs (and opening the page) keeps focus on the chosen tab; a library's tabs are core `pages/LibraryTabs` (`LibraryTab`/`LibraryTabKind`: Recommended, All, Genres, Collections, Unwatched, Studios, Decades and the user's own; `resolve` keeps the saved order and adds kinds it doesn't mention, `shown` always leaves one), saved per library with `DisplaySettingsStore.libraryTabs`, arranged in Settings → Display → Pages (`PageSetupScreen` lists Home and each library; both open the same frame, `SetupPage`: Theme first (`ThemeItem`, takes focus once, after it's built), then Tabs with the same `TabActions` (show/hide, rows, move, rename, remove) and "Add your own tab": `HomeSetupScreen` (Home always shows, its rows via `PageEditor`; then the user's custom pages, switch = pinned) and `LibraryTabsScreen`), never on the page; row tabs are `RowsViewModel` pages keyed by `LibraryTabs.pageKey` (Recommended keeps `PageKeys.library`), Genres/Studios/Decades rows from `LibraryTabs.rows` (`MediaServer.studios` = `Studios?parentId`), Unwatched is `BrowseLibrary(viewKey, startWith)`; Fast-forward/Rewind on a row (and in the Edit page's list and row menu, which also has an "Artwork style" line) step its `RowStyle` (`step`), named for a moment (focus waits on a holder in the row while its cards are swapped, then goes into the new style, asked again until a card has it, since some styles lay out a beat later; quick presses step on from the look asked for (`pendingLook`), not the saved one, and only that look takes focus; a guard hands focus back after 10 s if the look never arrives (a slow save is waited for); and the whole row is brought back into view); the All view's A–Z bar goes where `DisplayPrefs.tvLetterBar` says (`LetterBarPlace`: Automatic = the side away from the rail, left, right, top, off). Its stops are `core/pages/MenuStops` ids arranged by the user (`tvMenuStops`, `tvDialStops` with `ring * 4 + spoke` slots; Settings always stays) in `MenuStopsScreen`, built into entries by `menuCatalog` (`MenuCatalog.stops(prefs)`/`dial(prefs)`/`entry(id, prefs)`); settings, tools and sign-out sit in `SettingsHubScreen`. Groups (core `pages/MenuGroups`: `MenuGroup` id/name/stops in `DisplayPrefs.tvMenuGroups`, shown as one stop `group:<id>` where its first member was, members not shown alone and labelled without the group's words, `memberLabel`) open in place (`NavEntry.children`): the overlay as a small grid of their own in its card (above), the rail lists the members indented under it, the bar inline after it, the dial as a list in its centre; OK opens, focus goes to the first member, moving off or Back folds it, and the current page's group shows open with its stop marked; folding waits a moment after focus leaves the group (rail) or bar (`inGroup`/`inBar`), since handing focus from the group to its first member passes through "nothing focused here", which used to fold it at once and drop focus (closing the rail; `railGroupOpensOnOk`). Each stop is In the menu / In More / Hidden (core `StopPlace`, `MenuLayout.main`/`more`/`move`/`setPlace`): More's stops (`tvMoreStops`, null = `MenuStops.defaultMore`: Collections and Playlists libraries, Comics, Channels, Your pages, Switch user; the old Watch now together stop, `MenuStops.TOGETHER`, is no longer shown and a saved arrangement holding it skips it) sit on the More page (`MoreRoute`, `home/MoreScreen`, large tiles inside `MainMenu(MenuStops.MORE)`), reached by a "More" stop (`MenuStops.MORE`) just before Settings while More holds anything (the overlay shows them as its pop-up's More section instead, with no More stop); a page kept in More marks More in the menu (`MenuCatalog.stops`), so the menu opens there. Your pages (`PagesRoute`, `pages/PagesScreen`) is a top-level page like the others: inside `MainMenu(MenuStops.PAGES)`, laid out like More (heading, tiles in a grid: New page, then each page, "A tab on Home" under pinned ones), focus on its first tile once the grid has placed it; Search, Home and Settings never go there (`MenuLayout.NEVER_MORE`). `MenuStopsScreen` (`MenuStopsEditor`): suggested groups first (`MenuGroups.suggest`: libraries sharing leading words, "Make “Anime”" / Not now = `tvGroupSuggestionsDismissed`), then In the menu (numbered), In More, Hidden (with the overlay or the rail, Search, Settings and Switch user aren't offered and a line says they're in the top-right corner, or for the overlay in the menu's top row; moving a stop steps over them, `MenuLayout.move(notShown)`); a stop's choices: Move up/down, its place, and for a group Rename, Change libraries, Split up; a playlist stop Remove from the menu; on the dial the same list is a panel ("Groups, More and hidden"), groups placeable on spokes. Easy mode keeps groups and More (`MenuStops.essential`) and the profile corner or the pop-up's avatar (its way to Settings); the overlay has no Customize tile there. Playlists are pinned from their page's More (TV `PlaylistScreen(pinning)`): "Pin to Home" (`DisplayPrefs.homePlaylists` id → name, a Home tab after the pinned pages, `HomeScreen(playlistPage)` showing the playlist's page under the tabs, tab id `playlist:<id>`; unpinned there or in Settings → Display → Pages → Home) and "Add to the menu" (`menuPlaylists`, stop `playlist:<id>`); deleting the playlist drops both. Music libraries have a Playlists tab (`LibraryTabKind.PLAYLISTS`, `LibraryTabs.resolve(…, music)`, shown by default, also after a saved order): the user's music playlists as a grid. Dialogs opened by a long press use `ignoreHeldSelect()` so the held OK button doesn't click their first option. TV art rows draw at a design size and shrink with `designScale` by `RowSize`. Poster, wide and banner rows take `TV_CARD_SCALE`/`TV_BANNER_SCALE` of `RowSize`'s (phone-sized) heights (`tvPosterDp`/`tvWideDp`/`tvBannerDp`), pictures still asked for at full size so prefetch matches; the focused-title summary is 148 dp and follows the caption size (the focused row's own `RowDisplay.captionSize`, else the page's: Compact leaves the description out, Minimal also shows the logo for the name); stacked has no caption corner, so Customize's layout leaves that choice out there. `RowsPage` shows rows one at a time by default (`PageArrangement.ROWS`; `STACKED` is the stacked layout, Display → Arrangement): the row in use sits along the bottom (`RowAtBottom`; the list has a screen's room above its rows so any row can; the row in use is placed again whenever it changes size, e.g. a cards-hidden row growing to the screen's height, which is given explicitly since the list's own height excludes that room; only the row's own request (`placeRow`) moves the page, a card's never does (art cards report their design size, which pulled the row up), so nothing jumps); cards-hidden rows send ◀▶ off their ends straight to the menu (`LocalMenuEdge`); the tabs taking focus put the first row back in its place (`animateScrollToItem(0, roomAbove)`: the search for something above it could leave the list part-scrolled); no page has an Edit page button any more (`RowsPage(editButton)` defaults off; Settings → Display → Pages, or holding OK on a row); a row along the bottom keeps `RowFoot` under it and `CardRow` a few dp above and below its cards, so a focused card's growth stays on screen and the others are hidden until reached, so the backdrop and theme get the rest; no summary at the top: the focused title's `ArtCaption` (row and place as its label, logo off; `captionWidth`, narrow; `DisplayPrefs.tvCaptionSize` Normal/Compact = no description/Minimal = logo, no description, or the row's own `RowDisplay.captionSize`, the row look's "Caption": Page's/Normal/Compact/Minimal, used wherever a caption is drawn: rows one at a time, cards hidden, full art; the corner stays the page's) sits in the corner `DisplayPrefs.tvCaptionCorner` gives (core `CaptionCorner.resolve`; Alternate = left/right row by row), rows' own titles off there (`LocalRowTitle`), with `ArtCaptionShade` on its edge; each one-at-a-time row is a screen-tall layer laying out caption and cards by `tvRowPlace` (core `RowPlace`: along the bottom/top, or down the left/right as `ColumnCards`: posters two abreast, other styles wide cards, ▲▼ along it by hand (index by index, the column scrolled to the card first) and past its ends `onPastEnd` → `RowsPage.toRow`, which places the next/previous row and asks its `rowEntry` for focus, since the focus search from a lazy column to a row a screen away found nothing and fell to the menu; a gap between the column and a caption on the same side) and `tvRowExtent` (Full / Part way, `PartialShare`), the row starting beside the caption (so part way) whenever they share a line or side; Full art and cards-hidden rows take the same corner and size; Left at a row's start goes straight to the menu (`LocalMenuEdge`, since the tabs could be "nearest" with the caption beside the row); an active showcase is scrolled past the room above so its top meets the list's; the tabs keep their place and fade; Left/Right never leave a row except towards the menu (the row's `focusProperties.exit`); moving Up/Down back into a row lands on the card last focused in it (`RowsPage` `rowLast`, the row's `focusProperties.enter` redirects to that card's requester, never requests and cancels, which sent the Up on to the tabs; nearest-on-screen used to depend on the other row's card sizes); Home's page (`RowsPage(startOnRows = true)`) is entered at the card left, else the first row, so opening Home or coming back to it (Back from a library) never lands on its tabs, while library pages open on their tab; the page's last card and each row's (`lastCard`, `rowLast`) are saved state (`rememberSaveable`), so Back from a details page puts focus on the card left (RowsPage asks for it until it has it); Home's and a library's first focus (`focusedOnce`, `opened`) are saved too, so coming back never re-focuses the start (a library page that can't restore, e.g. a grid, gets its current tab after 1.5 s; Home with no card to return to, its first row); Back on a library tab other than the first goes to the first tab, and on a pinned page to Home's tab, before leaving; coming back from the menu focus returns to the card left, not the tabs (`rows/FocusReturn`: `MainMenu` asks the page through `LocalFocusReturn`, `RowsPage` notes the last card and gives it the requester via `LocalReturnTarget`/`returnFocus(id)` on every card kind); library pages have no Edit page button (`RowsPage(editButton = false)`, Settings → Display → Pages does it); stacked, the row in use always takes one place at the top of the list, title and all, right under the focused-title summary, with the rows after it below and the ones above gone whole (never part shown under the summary): `placeAtTop` scrolls the list to it (started again when the card's own bring-into-view interrupts it, which as a merged request used to place the card instead), the list has room below its last row so the last rows come up there too, and cards' own requests (`RowAtTop`) only scroll what's out of view; rows inside keep the TV behaviour. Page tabs (Home's pinned pages, a library's tabs and Albums/Artists) are `ui/components/PageTabs` in the user's `DisplayPrefs.tvTabStyle` (core `TabStyle`: chips, underline, sliding pill, large words, frosted glass, neon sign, cinema marquee, film strip, ink brush, ticket stubs; each keeps open and focused apart; picked in Display → Tabs via `TabStylePicker`, every style drawn live; tabs are always focusable and choose on OK's release). The page's tabs (`RowsPage(header)`) sit at the very top, above the focused-title summary, while on them or the first row, and slide away further down. The focused-title summary keeps a gap above the rows (2-line description); `FilmStripRow` is clipped to the page (never under the menu rail), and its Up/Down search with the other (tilted, so slightly higher or lower) frames switched off, so they always change rows and only ◀▶ move along it and shows no caption of its own there (`caption = false`), since the summary already names the focused title.
- Customize (setting pages and rows up from the page itself): TV holding OK on a row (`RowsPage`/`FullArtPage`, when the page's rows are known through `pages/LocalPageRows`) opens `pages/CustomizePanel`, a side panel down the right with the page live beside it (no dim), held by the page frame (`ThemedPage`'s `CustomizeHost`, `LocalCustomizeHost`, so it outlives the page's redraws, e.g. rows ↔ full art); two sections: This row (`rowLookItems`, shared with `RowSettingsDialog`; Move up/down, Rename, Hide through the `RowsViewModel`) and This page in groups: View (only on a tab with a view choice, first, focus on the choice in use: see below), What's on it (Rows → `PageEditor`, Tabs → `HomeSetupScreen`/`LibraryTabsScreen`), How it looks (theme, background, tab style where the page has tabs), How it's laid out (`layoutItems`, with "Use this layout on every page" / "Same as every page"), and Every page (What's shown, opening `DisplaySettingsScreen(page = SHOWN)`). When the panel closes, `RowsPage` puts focus back on the row's card left (`rowEntry`, asked until the page has it): Android hands it back to the card that had it, which a new style had replaced, so it fell to the menu. A tab's view lives in Customize → View, not on the page (there is no View button): the tab showing tells the page frame's `CustomizeHost.view` its `TabViewChoice` through `ReportTabView` while it shows (the All and Unwatched tabs from `BrowseLibrary`, Collections and music Playlists from `CollectionsGrid`, Genres/Studios/Decades/own tabs and Home's pinned pages from `RowsOrTitles`; cleared when the tab goes, so Recommended and Home itself have no View), with its name, Rows (tabs of rows only), the view and its columns, and how to save them, through the tab's own saving under the same keys as before (`browse/<lib>`, `browse/<lib>/unwatched`, `browse/<lib>/collections`, `browse/<lib>/playlists` as `BrowseOptions.view`/`columns`; `rows/<pageKey>` as `TabView`); the panel offers Rows, Posters, Artwork, List and the show views as chips, then Columns for a grid (`columnChoices`), and the tab redraws behind it at once. Back is taken as a key on the panel (closes it). When the panel closes after the view changed, focus goes to the tab's `refocus` (Sort on All/Unwatched, the chosen tab elsewhere) so it's never left on nothing. Not in easy mode (no Customize there). Saves from pickers that close on the choosing press go through `changePrefs`, which runs on `AppContainer.saveScope` so closing never cancels them. Named layouts: core `LayoutPreset` (Cinematic, Classic rows, Showcase, Compact, Minimal; each a full `PageLook`), "Look" first in Display → Layout for every page (`PageLooks.applyEverywhere`, drops pages' own) and in each page's layout (`applyToPage`, Customize and Pages); `LayoutPreset.matching` names the current one, else "Your own" once fine-tuned. Per-page layouts are core `pages/PageLook`/`PageLooks` (arrangement, caption corner/size, row place/length, tab style, full art's Up/Down, focus style, card corners and spacing) in `DisplayPrefs.pageLooks` by top-level page key (`ownerOf`: a library's tabs go with the library, Home's pinned pages with Home): only what differs from every page's layout (the `tv*` fields) is stored; `forPage` gives a page its layout (applied by `ThemedPage` to `LocalDisplayPrefs`, and by Home and the library route before their frame), `useEverywhere` makes it every page's (clearing the others), `reset` drops it. Anything inside a page that saves display settings must save the stored ones (`changePrefs`/`rememberStoredPrefs`), never the page's view of them. Settings → Display shows "Layout for every page"; each page's setup (Settings → Display → Pages) has a Layout item (`LayoutItem`) with the same choices. Phone: a row header's style label opens `CustomizeSheet` (This row: style, size, cards, titles, ink, move, rename, hide; This page: Edit rows) when the page provides `LocalPagePanel`; the phone has no per-page layout.
- Row look options (TV, `RowDisplay`, in `rowLookItems` so the Customize panel and the row dialog both have them): "Load more at the end" (`loadMore`, offered when the row has a `MediaRow.more`: a `RowMore` = the request that made it, opaque outside `core/jellyfin` (`rowMore`; resume, Next Up, sorted lookups incl. "Latest in…" films/shows, and `MediaServer.moreOfQuery` for genre/studio/decade and custom query rows; none for random ones or codec-filtered queries); TV `rows/rememberLoadMore` grows the row by `MediaServer.moreOfRow(more, startIndex, MORE_BATCH)` as focus comes within 5 cards of its end, until a short batch), "Wrap round" (`wrapRound`, poster/wide card rows: ◀ from the first card to the last and ▶ from the last to the first, only once a loading row is complete), "Titles show: On the focused card" (`titlesOnFocus`, the room kept so nothing moves) and "Logos on the pictures" (`logoCards`, wide rows: `MediaCard(logoUrl)` over a soft shade instead of words under it, words when it won't load). Progress on cards: core `ProgressStyle` Bar / Circle / None (`DisplayPrefs.progressStyle`, read through `progress`, which follows the old `progressBars` switch until chosen; `withProgress` keeps `progressBars` in step so None turns bars off on details pages and in the player too), What's shown on both apps; TV `ProgressCircle` and the phone card's ring in the bottom corner.
- No wash over pages' pictures: every page draws its picture whole (`PageBackdropSlot(clean = true)`, stacked rows too, with `ArtCaptionShade(top)` behind the summary); the caption box (`DisplayPrefs.tvCaptionBox`, off by default; Customize → Every page, Display → Layout for every page) puts `ArtCaption` and the stacked summary on a dark rounded box (`CaptionBoxColor`) and drops the edge shades. A library's full lists (All and Unwatched, `BrowseLibrary`) look like Home: the focused title's picture (else the one last focused, else the first) behind the page through `PageBackdropSlot(clean = true)`, so the page's theme and chosen background (smart colour and the rest) work as on Home, with `GridShade` over a picture (light behind the details, darker under the posters); the tabs bar in Home's place and `TabStyle` with Sort and Filters (and Clear filters, the admin's selecting buttons) as small `BarButton`s at its end and the count as small text beside them (the view is chosen in Customize → View); the grid's cards as Home's rows (`MediaCard`: the page's focus style, corners, spacing, `cardTitles`, watched/progress marks; posters bare with the details naming the focused one, artwork and album covers with their words, ticks in the names while selecting), the List view's rows see-through with the page's corners; the A–Z bar slim and dimmed until it has focus. The layout is `DisplayPrefs.tvGridLayout` (core `GridLayout`, per page through `PageLook.gridLayout`, a library's tabs taking their library page's; "Library list" in Customize → Layout on a library page and in Display → Layout for every page): Fixed grid (default) holds the bar and the focused title's `ArtCaption` (the library's name and place as its label, `captionLogo`) at the top and scrolls the posters beneath (their top fading under the details); Scrolling grid has no details block, the posters fill the page and the bar is the grid's first line (laid out to the page's edge with `bleed`), scrolling away with them, a poster's name showing under it while focused. ▼ from the tabs bar goes into the grid at the title last used, else the first in sight (`gridEntry`; the A–Z bar beside it used to be "nearer"), ▲ from the first line goes to Sort (scrolled back into sight first) and ▲ from the buttons to the chosen tab (`tabFocus`), ▼ on the last line stays put once everything is loaded, a focused title comes whole into sight with room round it (`WholeInSight`), and Back deep in the grid (taken as a key) or a held ▲ goes to the first title. The show views (Rolodex, Bouncing box and the rest) keep their own look under the same tabs bar. A custom page opened from the menu (`CustomPageScreen`) is laid out like Home: its own layout (`PageLooks.forPage`, full art with the menu over it, else `RowsPage` under its name), no button bar (its rows through Customize; name, pin and delete in Settings → Display → Pages; the buttons only while it's empty).
- TV page extras (Display → Layout for every page; Customize → Every page / How it's laid out): focus style (core `FocusStyle` Grow / Outline / Glow / Lift, `DisplayPrefs.tvFocusStyle`, per page as `PageLook.focusStyle`; `MediaCard` takes `cardFocusLook`'s scale, frame and glow; art rows keep their own), "Which row this is" (`DisplayPrefs.tvRowMarker`, on by default: `components/RowMarker` "3 / 9" on a slim track, bright for a moment after the row changes, on rows one at a time and full art, on the side away from the menu unless a column of cards runs down it), holding ▲ on a row goes straight to the first row (the first press moves one row, the repeats are taken: `RowsPage` → `toRow(0)`, `FullArtPage` → `shown[0]`), card corners and spacing (core `CardCorners` Rounded/Square and `CardSpacing` Tight/Normal/Roomy, `DisplayPrefs.tvCardCorners`/`tvCardSpacing`, per page as `PageLook.cardCorners`/`cardSpacing`: `MediaCard`'s shape and its focus frame follow the corners, `CardRow`'s gap and `ColumnCards`' (×0.8) the spacing), the remote's Channel and Page keys stepping through a page's tabs from anywhere on it (`components/tabKeys`/`tabStep` on `HomeScreen` and `LibraryScreen`: Channel down/Page up previous, Channel up/Page down next, nothing past the ends; the tab change is the same as choosing it), and a clock (`DisplayPrefs.tvClock`, off by default: `PageClock` top end in `ThemedPage`, the device's 12/24-hour format; `LocalClockRoom` makes full art's tabs and a top-end caption move aside).
- Custom pages pinned to Home are Home's tabs on TV ("Home" then each pinned page, the same tab strip as library pages: top right on full art via `FullArtPage(header)`, which leaves keys to the tabs while they have focus; `HomeScreen` `PinnedPage`, focus kept on the chosen tab through the page's redraws), not menu stops by default. Pages (Home `PageKeys.HOME`, library overviews `PageKeys.library(id)`, custom pages `PageKeys.custom(id)`) are assembled by `RowsViewModel` from built-in rows plus the user's `PageLayout` (core `pages/`): order, hidden, renamed and custom rows (`RowSource` query/collection/playlist). Layouts, custom pages and `DisplayPrefs` live in `DisplaySettingsStore` (in the device's display profile, see below) and sync like other display settings. Spoiler hiding: `isSpoiler` blurs unwatched episodes' stills; `hidesDescription` holds back descriptions of unwatched episodes and movies (details pages offer "Show"). Anything shown about an item (ratings, overview, badges, titles, progress...) must respect `DisplayPrefs` via each app's `LocalDisplayPrefs`; use `DisplayPrefs.facts(item)` for fact lines.
- TV ambient screensaver: `DisplayPrefs.tvAmbientMinutes` (0 = off, default 5); `MainActivity.dispatchKeyEvent` feeds `ui/ambient/TvIdle`, and `onStart`/`onStop` tell it the app is shown/hidden (`TvIdle.visible`: it never comes on in the background, leaving closes it, coming back counts as a press); the nav host shows `AmbientScreen` (full-screen dialog: slow drift, clock, caption alternating corners) outside the player/recap, built from `homeRows` by core `ambient/Ambient.slides` (continue → next up → new; one per show; captions "S2 · E5", never episode names); OK opens details, any other key leaves; theme music stops.
- TV home screen: `core/rows/HomeChannelContent` decides Play Next vs. channel items from Home rows; `tv/channels/HomeChannels` writes them with androidx.tvprovider (WorkManager + after playback) and links back with `nitrate://item|play/<id>` (`TvLinks`). `MainActivity` turns links, ACTION_SEARCH and the search key into `TvRequest`s that `NitrateNavHost` carries out once signed in.
- TV player's group button (`player/GroupPanel`, key `with` kept; `groupButtonLabel`): one `PlayerButton` after Subtitles (where the With… button was; the separate Group chat button is gone) for shared titles, Group Watch Sessions and an older app's live chat, saying the group's name; it opens `GroupPanel`, a side panel on the right with focus kept inside: the top line (Change → Counting for / We're in the same room), the chat line (→ Chats: Group chat on/off, New chat, the earlier chats dated YYYY/MM/DD, each with a heart button reached with ▶ from its line, spoken "Favourite"/"Not a favourite"), what was said, the title's quick reactions (rows of five, its own emojis first) and quick lines (characters' names and short phrases, ◀▶ along the row, one OK sends) and the message field (▼ reactions → lines → field, ▲ back), then Start a Group Watch Session (or Leave / End it for everyone). Back as a key goes back a page (focus back on the line that opened it), then is left to the player, which closes the panel with focus back on the button (`docs/features/partners.md`).
- TV quick profile switch: `NavEntry.onLongClick` (drawer, bar and dial all honour a held OK); "Switch user" long-press → `profiles/QuickSwitchDialog` (other profiles, PIN via `PinPad`, `AppContainer.switchAccount`, then Home).
- TV update offer (`components/UpdateDialog`, at app start and from Settings → Updates): "What's new" sits in `ReleaseNotes`, a box of its own height that rolls slowly through the notes by itself until ▲▼ take over (Up from Update now goes straight to it), so the choices always stay on screen.
