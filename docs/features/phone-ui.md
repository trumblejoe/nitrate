# Phone and tablet UI

The phone/tablet look and layout (mobile-ui common, so iOS too), widgets, notifications and the player's swipes.

Read CLAUDE.md first: its rules (keys, ids, privacy, licences, focus, server load) apply here too.

- Mobile look: screens sit in `TintedBackdrop` (tint from `rememberArtworkColor`), use `accentColor()` for highlights, `SectionHeader`, `PillButton`, `RoundAction`, and `pressable()` for touch feedback. Artwork that should morph into the page it opens gets a `sharedKey`/`sharedArtwork(key)` unique on its screen, passed on as `DetailsRoute.artKey`. Leave `NavBarClearance` at the bottom of tab screens for the floating nav pill. Rows render through `CinematicRow` (Posters/Artwork/Film strip/Lightning split/Cressida, `RowStyle.choices`; Showcase and Full Showcase are retired: the enum values stay so saved settings read, and `RowDisplay.normalized()` (applied in `RowsViewModel` and each app's row code) shows Showcase as Artwork and Full Showcase as Artwork with `cards = false`, the stored value untouched until the user changes it; `RowDisplay.stepped` is the Fast-forward/Rewind and Edit-page order, Artwork then Artwork with cards hidden (`styleLabel`). Artwork with its cards hidden (`cardsHidden`) is one title at a time: TV `rows/CardlessRow` (◀▶ steps, "row · n / N" label, wrap round and rotate artwork from `ShowcaseOptions`) reports its title's picture to `RowsPage`, which puts it in `PageBackdropSlot` so the page's background choice and theme layer over it, never its own image (stacked rows: a banner until focused, then the screen with the others stepped aside; one at a time every cards-hidden row is always a screen of its own, never a banner, since the rows either side slide past as the page moves (`cardsHiddenRowsNeverBanner`), and the backdrop shows the row's own title's picture until the row reports one); captions on full-screen pictures are `components/ArtCaption` over `ArtCaptionShade`, as the screensaver's: a small capitals label with the row and place, the title in bold or a modest logo, a quiet line or two, in the corner `tvCaptionCorner` gives; `DisplayPrefs.tvCaptionSize` Normal / Compact / Minimal (`captionLogo`: the logo instead of the name, facts, no description); phone `FullShowcaseRow` pager with `fullShowcaseImage` (poster upright, 1920px backdrop sideways; episodes always the show's backdrop) for cards hidden, "Show cards" in the row menu and page editor; chosen from the row header via `onDisplay`; the last three live in `ArtRows.kt` in each app and share geometry from core `RowArt` and drawing from core-android `art/RowArtDrawing`; Cressida's linework is `RowDisplay.ink` (`CressidaInk`) coloured by `inkTint`/`inkArgb` (Smart = core `InkPalette.smart` over the card's artwork pixels, via each app's `rememberInkColor`), drawn procedurally and deterministically there — keep designs original, no copied emblems or logos); the phone's Home passes `ArtworkFirst::display` as `RowsViewModel` defaults, so unstyled rows show artwork rather than posters. Screenshot tests use the abstract art from `tools/generate-sample-art.py`.
- Phone/tablet layout (mobile-ui common, so iOS too): from 840 dp wide (`wideDetails()`, `WIDE_DETAILS`; tablets, wide windows) a details page is two columns, the picture, title, facts and actions on the left (scrolling on its own) and the rows (season's episodes, collection, cast, extras, More like this) on the right; narrower, one list (the page is built from its parts `hero`/`facts`/`rows()` so both use the same code). The Settings tab is `SettingsTab`: on a phone `SettingsScreen` as before; wide, the sections on the left and the chosen page beside them (`LocalSettingsPane` makes `SettingsScreen`'s links, and Advanced/What's shown inside a page, open there instead of `SettingsPageRoute`; `LocalInSettingsPane` hides that page's back arrow). Single-column pages don't stretch: `ReadableWidth` (`BackScaffold` pages up to 1200 dp, a settings page on its own 900 dp), grids are adaptive already. A details page opened at a season (`seasonId` on a show) is that season's page: "<Show> ›" (opening the show), the season's name, "6 episodes · 3 watched", Play/tracks/Play on… for its next unwatched episode (else its first), its own overview if it has one, all following the season chips. While loading, pages show `Placeholders.kt` blocks in their own shape (`DetailsPlaceholder` (two columns when wide), `RowsPlaceholder` Home/library/custom pages, `ListPlaceholder` playlists, `GridPlaceholder` All/artists), still and read out as "Loading", never a bare spinner. Page actions look alike everywhere: a full-width primary `PillButton` for the likeliest thing, `RoundAction`s for the rest ending in More, and More (and long presses' menus) as a `ChoiceSheet` (label + optional line; `onChoose = {}` when a choice opens a dialog of its own) — the details page and the playlist page (Continue / Play from the start, From the start, Shuffle, More: Edit order, In story order, Rename, Delete; entries' long press) both.
- Phone player swipes (brightness left, volume right) must stay hard to trigger by accident: core `playback/SwipeLevel` decides starts (edges excluded, flicks and sideways moves rule the touch out) and `LevelDrag` the level (rests at 50%); `PlaybackSettings.swipeControls` turns them off.
- Player group button (mobile-ui common `WithChip.kt`: `rememberWatchCompany`, `GroupButton` (avatars and the group's name), `CountingBanner`; `GroupSheet.kt`: `PlayerGroupSheet`/`GroupSheet`, a bottom sheet with Change, the chat and Chats, and Group Watch Sessions; Android, iOS and desktop players put it in their top row in place of the old With… chip and chat button); easy mode shows who it counts for as words only (`docs/features/partners.md`).
- Phone home-screen widgets (`:mobile` `widgets/WatchWidgets`, Jetpack Glance): Continue watching and Next up (core `widgets/WidgetCards`, `WidgetKind`: Home's `home/continue_watching`/`home/next_up` rows, one per show, at most 6; a card's line is "S2 · E5" or the year, never an episode's name; `spokenTitle` for screen readers); pictures 320×180 from `backdropUrl(item, 480)`; the cards and pictures kept in `cache/widgets` and shown when the server can't be reached; restores the sign-in when started with the app closed ("Open Nitrate to sign in" without one). Refreshed by the system every 30 minutes and on `MainActivity.onStop` (`WatchWidgets.refresh`). A title's tap (`playIntent`, `EXTRA_PLAY_ITEM`, a unique `nitrate://play/<id>` per title) reaches `MobileApp` as `openItem` = `PLAY_ITEM_PREFIX` + id and plays through the guarded path, resuming; the header opens the app. Tested with Glance's `runGlanceAppWidgetUnitTest` (`WatchWidgetsTest`).
- Group Watch Session reminders (phone; switch "Group Watch Session reminders" in Notifications, on until switched off, Android channel `watch_party`; see `docs/features/partners.md`): no Google/Apple push or outside service. Android (`AndroidNewEpisodeNotifier.scheduleParties`): one WorkManager wake-up per group (tag `watch-party`, unique `party-<group>`, inexact, no exact-alarm permission; WorkManager keeps it across restarts) 15 minutes before its next party (`WatchParty.nextReminderAt`), set again whenever the groups load or a party changes (`AppContainer.partiesChanged`; never on TV) and cleared on signing out or switching. On waking (`PartyReminderWorker` → `remindParty`) it takes one quick look (`MediaServer.watchParty`): pushed back → wakes again for the new time; off or the user can't make it → nothing; else "Watch party with Tommy & Suzy · 8:00 · S1:E4" with Join (opens the app on the party, `EXTRA_OPEN_PARTY` → `MobileApp`'s `PARTY_PREFIX` → `AppContainer.openParty`), +15 min and Not tonight (`PartyActionReceiver`, answered without opening the app), then sets the next party's wake-up. A postponement or Not tonight waiting for the user's vote is notified from the 15-minute invite check (the same `partners()` look) with Yes/No buttons, once per request.
- New-episode and new-season notifications (phone; each its own switch and Android channel): which to announce is `core/notifications/NewEpisodes` (followed shows) and `NewSeasons` (first episode of season 1+ of a show followed or watched before, `MediaServer.watchedSeries`; names the season only, never episode titles or counts); `AppContainer.newEpisodes` (`NewEpisodeNotifier`) checks via WorkManager and on `LibraryChanged`, remembering the newest add seen per account; a show starting a season gets only the season notification. Taps reach `MainActivity` with `NewEpisodeNotifier.EXTRA_OPEN_ITEM`.

## Where the code is

- Settings: `mobile-ui/.../ui/SettingsScreen.kt` (the tab's list), the pages (`SettingsPageScreen` and their sections, toggles, plugin blocks) in `SettingsPages.kt`, and shared rows and dialogs (`Link`, `group`, `ScopeTag`, Quick Connect, Watch together, device report) in `SettingsRows.kt`.
- Watch groups on the phone and iPad (see `docs/features/partners.md`): "Watch something with someone" is the full-screen `GroupSetupSheet` (`GroupSetupPages`, a step per page, Back steps back); a long press on a Watching together card opens `TogetherChoicesSheet` (`TogetherChoicesList`); `CountingPrompt` asks "Just this group / All groups where it's next" as a sheet once the player has closed.
