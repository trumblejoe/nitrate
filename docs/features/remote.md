# Remote control, Cast and SyncPlay

The phone as a remote, Google Cast through Jellyfin's receiver, and SyncPlay.

Read CLAUDE.md first: its rules (keys, ids, privacy, licences, focus, server load) apply here too.

- Remote control: `AppContainer.remote` (`RemoteControlHub`) listens to `MediaServer.events()` and forwards commands to the player that registered itself as `remote.active`; each app handles `Play`/`DisplayMessage`/volume itself (TV `PlayerRoute.audioIndex`/`subtitleIndex` carry a Play's tracks into `PlaybackSession.load`). The phone is the remote: `MediaServer.controllableSessions` (`controllableByUserId`, which for an admin is everyone's) through core `remote/RemoteDevices` (only the signed-in account's own sessions, `userId`) (other devices, playing then Nitrate first, newest session per device); "Play on…" (`PlayOnDialog`, details page and player More) sends `playOn` with the tracks Play would start with and opens `RemoteScreen`; before anything is sent to play there (Play on…, the player's Play on, the remote's episodes), the phone runs the guards itself (`AppActions.checkBeforeSending` → core `PluginRegistry.checkBeforeSending`, over `checkBeforePlay`) and shows any question on the phone, sending only when they allow it (Not now sends nothing; "play this instead" sends that one; marking watched or a plugin's own step happens first). The TV handles an incoming remote Play as before, and nothing is added for Jellyfin's web app or other clients (seek, keys, volume, Audio/Subtitles via `setSessionTrack` = `Sessions/{id}/Command`); `PlayingElsewhereBar` above the nav pill while one of them plays (keyed on the account, so switching clears it). The remote (`RemoteScreen`) works on a `RemoteTarget` (`RemoteTargets.kt`: `SessionTarget` through the server, `CastTarget` for the Chromecast, `RemoteRoute(CAST_REMOTE)`, opened from the cast bar), shown as a `RemoteView`: the TV's question if any, poster and title, Skip intro/recap/credits from the server's segments (`PlaybackMoments.skippable`), a seek bar with trickplay previews, transport (icons with spoken names), tracks, volume, chapters (tap to go there) and the season's episodes (tap to play there; stills by `spoilerStill`); for a device that takes every `GeneralCommand.NAVIGATION` command (`ServerSession.navigable`, from its `SupportedCommands`) an "Arrows" pad (arrows, OK, Back, Home; the TV reports them via `MediaServer.acceptCommands` when `deviceKind` is TV, and `NitrateNavHost` presses them as keys, Home = `HomeRoute`). The TV's questions reach the phone through Jellyfin messages between the user's own sessions (core `remote/RemotePrompt`, headers `Nitrate.Prompt`/`Nitrate.PromptGone`/`Nitrate.Answer`, only to `PHONE_CLIENT` sessions of the same user, never shown as messages; `RemoteControlHub.offer`/`withdraw`/`answer`/`answers`/`promptFrom`): TV `player/AskPhones` repeats Up Next ("Play now"/"Not now"), "Still watching?" (Keep watching/Go back/Stop) and the credit-scene banner's button every `REPEAT_MS` while shown and acts on answers as on its own buttons; phones forget one after `FORGET_MS`, and `PlayingElsewhereBar` says "<TV> asks: …". Lock-screen and notification controls while another device plays (Android): mobile-ui `RemoteControlService` (a `MediaSessionService` around `RemoteSessionPlayer`, a `SimpleBasePlayer` mirroring the session, polled every 4 s, gone after a minute with nothing playing), started by `rememberRemoteFollower()` when the remote opens or Play on sends, its looking in app-core `remote/RemoteFollower`; iOS: Now Playing through mobile-ui `RemoteFollowing`.
- Google Cast (phone, Android; iOS bridged, its Swift side a draft in `iosApp/Drafts`): casting goes to Jellyfin's own receiver app (core `cast/Cast.RECEIVER_APP_ID` "F007D354", channel `urn:x-cast:com.connectsdk`), which plays straight from the server with the user's sign-in and reports as its own device. Messages are built and read by the server client (`MediaServer.castPlay(CastPlay)` "PlayNow" with items/start/tracks/version, `castCommand(CastCommand)` Pause/Unpause/Stop/Seek (ticks)/Next/Previous/SetVolume, `castStatus` reads `playbackstart`/`playbackprogress`/`playbackstop`; `core/jellyfin/JellyfinCast`). mobile-ui `platform/Casting.kt`: `CastControl` (`state`: devices, connected, status; `discover`/`connect`/`disconnect`/`send`) from `rememberCast()` (null on iOS and without Google Play services); Android `AndroidCast` over Google's Cast framework (`play-services-cast-framework`; `NitrateCastOptions` named in the manifest) with devices from the media router, listed in Nitrate's own Compose UI (no AppCompat `MediaRouteButton`). "Play on…" lists Chromecasts (`CastDeviceList`) beside the user's devices; while connected, Play anywhere (`go` in `MobileApp`, after the guards) casts instead (`castPlayFor`: a song with its album, else the item from where it was left with the user's tracks; `castTo`); `CastBar` above the nav pill (time · device, title, −10/+10 s, pause, stop casting). Tests use `FakeCast`.
- SyncPlay is no longer in the apps' main flow: "Watch now together" starts a Group Watch Session (`docs/features/partners.md`), and the TV menu's Watch now together stop and page, the TV player's More → Settings "Watch now together" page, the phone's Settings row and player More page (create/join a SyncPlay group, invite people) are gone (`MenuStops.TOGETHER` stays readable, not shown). Kept only for compatibility: `SyncPlayController` (fed by `RemoteControlHub`, `AppContainer.syncPlay`), `PlaybackSession.followGroup` and the apps' `syncPlay.openRequests` (a SyncPlay group this device is in, e.g. from an older Nitrate app's invite or another client), the Companion's watch-together invites (`WatchInvitePrompt`, `joinInvite`: a Group Watch Session's id joins that, else the older app's SyncPlay group), live chat and timeline notes in a SyncPlay group, and the Companion's `TogetherRecorder` counting SyncPlay groups played to the end.
- SyncPlay (compatibility): the logic is `core/syncplay/SyncPlayController` (clock, commands, ready/buffering, drift), fed by `RemoteControlHub` and shared as `AppContainer.syncPlay`. `PlaybackSession.followGroup` makes its public `player` (a `ForwardingPlayer`) turn play/pause/seek into group requests while in a group; the group's commands act on the session's private ExoPlayer. Apps open a player for `syncPlay.openRequests`.
