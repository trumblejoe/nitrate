# Remote control, Cast and SyncPlay

The phone as a remote, Google Cast through Jellyfin's receiver, and SyncPlay.

Read CLAUDE.md first: its rules (keys, ids, privacy, licences, focus, server load) apply here too.

- Remote control: `AppContainer.remote` (`RemoteControlHub`) listens to `MediaServer.events()` and forwards commands to the player that registered itself as `remote.active`; each app handles `Play`/`DisplayMessage`/volume itself (TV `PlayerRoute.audioIndex`/`subtitleIndex` carry a Play's tracks into `PlaybackSession.load`). The phone is the remote: `MediaServer.controllableSessions` (`controllableByUserId`, which for an admin is everyone's) through core `remote/RemoteDevices` (only the signed-in account's own sessions, `userId`) (other devices, playing then Nitrate first, newest session per device); "Play on…" (`PlayOnDialog`, details page and player More) sends `playOn` with the tracks Play would start with and opens `RemoteScreen`; before anything is sent to play there (Play on…, the player's Play on, the remote's episodes), the phone runs the guards itself (`AppActions.checkBeforeSending` → core `PluginRegistry.checkBeforeSending`, over `checkBeforePlay`) and shows any question on the phone, sending only when they allow it (Not now sends nothing; "play this instead" sends that one; marking watched or a plugin's own step happens first). The TV handles an incoming remote Play as before, and nothing is added for Jellyfin's web app or other clients (seek, keys, volume, Audio/Subtitles via `setSessionTrack` = `Sessions/{id}/Command`); `PlayingElsewhereBar` above the nav pill while one of them plays (keyed on the account, so switching clears it; looked for every minute while nothing plays, every 5 s while something does, and again whenever the app comes back into view, `AppContainer.appShown`). The remote (`RemoteScreen`) works on a `RemoteTarget` (`RemoteTargets.kt`: `SessionTarget` through the server, `CastTarget` for the Chromecast, `RemoteRoute(CAST_REMOTE)`, opened from the cast bar), shown as a `RemoteView`: the TV's question if any, poster and title, Skip intro/recap/credits from the server's segments (`PlaybackMoments.skippable`), a seek bar with trickplay previews, transport (icons with spoken names), tracks, volume, chapters (tap to go there) and the season's episodes (tap to play there; stills by `spoilerStill`); for a device that takes every `GeneralCommand.NAVIGATION` command (`ServerSession.navigable`, from its `SupportedCommands`) an "Arrows" pad (arrows, OK, Back, Home; the TV reports them via `MediaServer.acceptCommands` when `deviceKind` is TV, and `NitrateNavHost` presses them as keys, Home = `HomeRoute`). Choosing from the phone (Nitrate on a TV only: it also reports `GeneralCommand.CHOOSING` = GoToSearch, DisplayContent, SendString, `AppContainer` → `acceptCommands`; `ServerSession.choosable`, `RemoteView.choosable`): the remote shows "Choose for <TV>" and Type at the top, and with the arrows open (or nothing playing, so nothing is interrupted) a Go to row (Home = GoHome, Search = GoToSearch, each library = `MediaServer.showOn`, Jellyfin's DisplayContent with ItemId/ItemType/ItemName; the socket hands a DisplayContent's ItemId over dashed). The TV (`NitrateNavHost`) opens Search, a library (`CollectionFolder`/`UserView` → `LibraryRoute`), a playlist or a details page. Type (`MediaServer.typeOn`, SendString, an inline field on the remote) replaces the text of the TV's `TvTextField` in use (`RemoteTyping`, while one has focus), else opens Search for it (in place of a search already open). Choose for <TV> (mobile-ui `ChooseForTv`) returns to the phone's own pages with `ChoosingBanner` over them (not over the remote or a player): "Choosing for <TV>", Show on TV on a details page (`showOn`, the TV opens that title's page for the room), Done. While it's on, every Play in `MobileApp.guarded` (after a show or season becomes its episode) goes to the TV instead (`sendToChosenTv`: the sending guards asked on the phone, `checkedForSending`, then `playOn` with the tracks Play would start with and the chosen version, from where it was left, the start or a given time), ends choosing and opens that TV's remote; a book still opens here. A Group Watch Session from the remote (Nitrate on a TV, Companion feature `sessions`, the user in a watch group): `RemoteSessionCard` under Choose/Type (not with the arrows open). With none going, "Start a Group Watch Session" asks who (the user's groups), then starts Choose for <TV> with that group (`ChooseForTv.Target.groupId`, banner "Group Watch Session with Suzy on Living room TV"); Play then sends `RemoteSessionStart` (header `Nitrate.StartSession`, {groupId, itemId}, `RemoteControlHub.askToStartSession`, like the TV's questions between the user's own sessions) and opens the remote. The TV (`RemoteControlHub.sessionStarts` → `NitrateNavHost`) starts it as host (`AppContainer.startWatchingNow(gatherMinutes = GroupWatchSession.REMOTE_GATHER_MINUTES)`, 15: the Companion's `SessionPost.GatherMinutes`, at most `WatchSessions.MaxGather` 30, handed over as `WatchSessionState.GatherFor`; older logic hand-overs gather the usual minute) and plays it through its guards (in place of a player already open); a refusal shows on the TV. While one the user is in gathers or plays (`watchSessions()`, every 3 s), the card says what and where it stands (core `SessionStatus.line`: "Waiting for Suzy · Kim is in · starts by itself in 12 min", "Watching with Kim"), with the starter's Start now (`startWatchSessionNow`) and End for everyone (asked first). Sessions are per account, so the phone manages the one its TV hosts. Asking someone else to start one ("Ask … to watch together") is in the watch group choices on both apps (docs/features/partners.md). The TV's questions reach the phone through Jellyfin messages between the user's own sessions (core `remote/RemotePrompt`, headers `Nitrate.Prompt`/`Nitrate.PromptGone`/`Nitrate.Answer`, only to `PHONE_CLIENT` sessions of the same user, never shown as messages; `RemoteControlHub.offer`/`withdraw`/`answer`/`answers`/`promptFrom`): TV `player/AskPhones` repeats Up Next ("Play now"/"Not now"), "Still watching?" (Keep watching/Go back/Stop) and the credit-scene banner's button every `REPEAT_MS` while shown and acts on answers as on its own buttons; phones forget one after `FORGET_MS`, and `PlayingElsewhereBar` says "<TV> asks: …". Lock-screen and notification controls while another device plays (Android): mobile-ui `RemoteControlService` (a `MediaSessionService` around `RemoteSessionPlayer`, a `SimpleBasePlayer` mirroring the session, polled every 4 s, gone after a minute with nothing playing), started by `rememberRemoteFollower()` when the remote opens or Play on sends, its looking in app-core `remote/RemoteFollower`; iOS: Now Playing through mobile-ui `RemoteFollowing`.
- Google Cast (phone, Android; iOS bridged, its Swift side a draft in `iosApp/Drafts`): casting goes to Jellyfin's own receiver app (core `cast/Cast.RECEIVER_APP_ID` "F007D354", channel `urn:x-cast:com.connectsdk`), which plays straight from the server with the user's sign-in and reports as its own device. Messages are built and read by the server client (`MediaServer.castPlay(CastPlay)` "PlayNow" with items/start/tracks/version, `castCommand(CastCommand)` Pause/Unpause/Stop/Seek (ticks)/Next/Previous/SetVolume, `castStatus` reads `playbackstart`/`playbackprogress`/`playbackstop`; `core/jellyfin/JellyfinCast`). mobile-ui `platform/Casting.kt`: `CastControl` (`state`: devices, connected, status; `discover`/`connect`/`disconnect`/`send`) from `rememberCast()` (null on iOS and without Google Play services); Android `AndroidCast` over Google's Cast framework (`play-services-cast-framework`; `NitrateCastOptions` named in the manifest) with devices from the media router, listed in Nitrate's own Compose UI (no AppCompat `MediaRouteButton`). "Play on…" lists Chromecasts (`CastDeviceList`) beside the user's devices; while connected, Play anywhere (`go` in `MobileApp`, after the guards) casts instead (`castPlayFor`: a song with its album, else the item from where it was left with the user's tracks; `castTo`); `CastBar` above the nav pill (time · device, title, −10/+10 s, pause, stop casting). Tests use `FakeCast`.
- SyncPlay is no longer in the apps' main flow: "Start a Group Watch Session" starts one (`docs/features/partners.md`), and the TV menu's old Watch now together stop and page, the TV player's old More → Settings "Watch now together" page, the phone's Settings row and player More page (create/join a SyncPlay group, invite people) are gone (`MenuStops.TOGETHER` stays readable, not shown). Kept only for compatibility: `SyncPlayController` (fed by `RemoteControlHub`, `AppContainer.syncPlay`), `PlaybackSession.followGroup` and the apps' `syncPlay.openRequests` (a SyncPlay group this device is in, e.g. from an older Nitrate app's invite or another client), the Companion's watch-together invites (`WatchInvitePrompt`, `joinInvite`: a Group Watch Session's id joins that, else the older app's SyncPlay group), live chat and timeline notes in a SyncPlay group, and the Companion's `TogetherRecorder` counting SyncPlay groups played to the end.
- SyncPlay (compatibility): the logic is `core/syncplay/SyncPlayController` (clock, commands, ready/buffering, drift), fed by `RemoteControlHub` and shared as `AppContainer.syncPlay`. `PlaybackSession.followGroup` makes its public `player` (a `ForwardingPlayer`) turn play/pause/seek into group requests while in a group; the group's commands act on the session's private ExoPlayer. Apps open a player for `syncPlay.openRequests`.

- Chat: while the TV plays something with group chat, the remote has a Chat card (which conversation, the last messages, emojis, quick lines, the phone's keyboard, Turn off/Turn on for that playback), relayed through the TV, which posts as itself (`docs/features/partners.md`).
