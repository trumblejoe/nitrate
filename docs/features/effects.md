# Effects, page themes, pre-rolls and verses

TV effects: card effects, page themes and occasions, backdrops, pre-rolls and the public-domain verses.

Read CLAUDE.md first: its rules (keys, ids, privacy, licences, focus, server load) apply here too.

- Effects (TV; per display profile, synced): core `effects/Effects.kt`. Row level in `RowDisplay` (`clickEffect`: spin/bounce/wobble/pop/flip/jelly played before the card opens; `cardSpark` + `sparkRate`: lightning/fireworks/sparkles/embers/shimmer on a random composed card now and then), set in the row's settings (`RowSettingsDialog`, so also Edit page → Look); tv `ui/effects/CardEffects` (`rememberCardFx` per row in `RowsPage`, `LocalCardFx`, `Modifier.cardFx(id)` on `MediaCard` and the art rows; each spark goes off on 3–5 of the cards shown at once (`AT_ONCE`, starts staggered); cards-hidden rows and Full art (`FullArtPage` takes its row's `rememberCardFx`) take them on the title showing only: sparks over the whole screen, `cardFx(id, click = false)`, drawn at the screen's scale when the area is screen-sized (`drawSparkAnySize` → `drawScreenSpark`: a strike lighting the picture, fireworks across the sky, glints all over, embers from the bottom, a broad sheen; sizes and the bolt's strokes (`lightning(scale)`) follow the screen's height; a large art card keeps the card's spark; `ScreenSparkTest`); the click on its caption, `cardFx(id, spark = false)`; `holdAt` freezes sparks for screenshots). Page themes (`PageTheme`, `DisplayPrefs.pageThemes` by page key, `PageThemes.pageKeyFor(stop)`) chosen in Pages (`PageThemePicker`, live behind the list) and the day's `Occasion` (`Occasions.active`: most specific enabled first, Festivus > Christmas > winter; Easter by computus, Thanksgiving/Labor Day by weekday) drawn by `ui/effects/Scenes` `EffectsLayer` on every `MainMenu` page (through `ThemedPage(pageKey, insets)`, which also draws the page's backdrop, so both live as long as the frame and tab switches never restart them; More from pages use `ThemedPage(PageKeys.MORE_FROM)` directly), kept clear of the menu's insets, in depth layers (`Depth.kt`: scenes mark every drawing `back {}`/`mid {}`/`front {}`, `Pass` picks which run on the drawing thread, none set = all, for previews/tests/pre-rolls; shared particle helpers `rain`/`realSnow(layered = true)` split far/middle/near themselves via `atDepth`): `BEHIND` (back + mid, plus clips/photos) is drawn by the frame over the page's backdrop and under the page: pages with a focused title report its picture with `PageBackdropSlot(url, clean)` (`LocalThemeBehind`; `RowsPage`, `FullArtPage`; `clean` = the caption sits in a corner with its own `ArtCaptionShade` (rows one at a time, full art), so the picture is drawn as the screensaver does, `CleanBackdrop`: centred, no wash; stacked rows and details pages keep `Backdrop`'s left and bottom wash for their text; with no frame, tests, the picture is drawn in place), `INFRONT` over the page; the backdrop itself is core `effects/PageBackdrop` per page key (`DisplayPrefs.pageBackdrops`: the title's picture by default, Smart gradient / Smart colour from its main colour via `rememberArtColor` (`InkPalette.smart`), fixed gradients, flat colours incl. black), chosen with the theme in Pages (`BackgroundItem`/`BackgroundChoice`; More from pages have their own theme and background there); the front stays light (sparse near particles and lens glows, never fills or text; `ThemeDrawTest.frontLayersStayLight` keeps every scene's front under 12% of the screen and something behind); behind the page a scene never washes the title's picture: no skies, vignettes or full-screen hazes (`skyWash` stops at `SKY_EDGE`, 12% down; cloud decks along the top, fog and mist along the bottom, sizes from the screen's height, not fixed pixels), and big shapes (skylines, ridges, castles, roses, meadows, the rift) keep to the edges and corners, so only small passing things cross the middle; `ThemeDrawTest.pictureStaysClear` holds every theme and occasion to a mean alpha of 0.08 over the picture's area (x 20–80%, y 10–60%) (never the player; time-based, no stored particles, `at` holds a moment for screenshots; playing, `DrawnScene` draws each frame in software on a background thread into rotating bitmaps (never the one on screen or waiting to be) at up to 1280 wide, up to ~30 fps, so the drawing code must stay thread-safe; `ThemeDrawTest` draws every theme and occasion across time). `PageThemePicker` (contents `PageThemeChoices`) opens scrolled to the theme in use, which takes focus itself once built. The look is realistic, light first (`Realism.kt`/`Realism2.kt`: cached radial `glow`s scaled into place, additive blending, depth layers, `noise`/`fbm`, fireworks with drag/gravity/trails, branched lightning lighting a cloud deck, cloth flags shaded by their folds); keep new scenes in that style. Scenes about real subjects (core `EffectClips`: safari, Army, Air Force, Navy, Marines, Veterans Day, Easter) play a looping film clip along the bottom (`ClipBand`: muted ExoPlayer into a TextureView, faded at its top edge) when the build ships `assets/fx/<name>.mp4`, else their drawing; clips come only from public-domain/CC0/CC BY(-SA) Commons footage via `tools/build-effect-clips.py` (or the manual `Effect clips` workflow, since Commons rate-limits cloud machines); Commons has refused both this container and GitHub's runners so far. The app's own photos (`assets/fx/photos/<set>/` + `photos.json`, cropped 16:9 and stripped of EXIF/GPS; taken by the app's author, credited as "Effect photos") are shown by `PhotoStage` (slow zoom/drift, crossfades, a random order each time it appears): Safari's `PhotoBand` when there's no clip (`EffectClips.photosForTheme`), and the Wild animals / Jellyfish pre-roll scenes (`PrerollScene.photos`, `usable(shipped)` with `photos/<set>` entries); `shippedPhotoSets` copes with asset managers that list nested files, credited in `fx/credits.json` on the licences page ("Effect footage"). `EffectsPrefs`: on, Reduce motion (still frame, no click/spark), amount, seasonal, per-occasion switches and dates (`occasionDates`, core `OccasionDates`: the usual dates, moving ones included, started `earlier`/ended `later` by up to `MAX_SHIFT` days, or the user's own `from`–`to` `MonthDay`s every year, running on past New Year when `to` comes before `from`; `Occasions.span(o, year, dates)`, `on`, `current`, `next`; `withDates(o, null)` goes back to the usual); Settings → Display → Effects (`EffectsSettingsScreen`, each occasion's dates (the user's when set), next date, Dates (`OccasionDatesDialog`: The usual dates with Start sooner/End later, or Dates of my own stepped by day ‹ › and month « », showing what it'll cover) and Preview). Off in easy mode. All drawings are original and done in code: no franchise names, logos or service emblems.
- Pre-rolls (TV; `DisplayPrefs.prerolls` = core `preroll/PrerollPrefs`, per display profile, off until switched on): before a film (or the first episode of a sitting, `Prerolls.SITTING_MS`) that starts from the top (never when resuming), `Prerolls.plan` gives `PrerollStep`s in order: a `PrerollScene` (its film clip `fx/<clip>.mp4` when shipped, else drawn: ocean floor, northern lights, starfield; clip-only scenes are offered only with their clip), a verse or the user's own line on black (fade in/out, tanka indentation), then videos: the server's intros (`MediaServer.intros` = `Items/{id}/Intros`, a cinema-mode plugin's) and one of the user's own from a chosen playlist/collection (`PrerollSource`). TV gate (`NitrateNavHost`): recap first when due, else `planPrerolls` (2.5 s limits) → `PrerollRoute`/`PrerollScreen` (OK skips one, Back skips all) → `playQueue(videos + title)`. Settings → While watching → Before it plays (`PrerollSettingsScreen`, Preview per verse/scene). Not in easy mode.
- Verses: core `poetry/Poems` — only public-domain translations (William N. Porter's 1909 Hyakunin Isshu, verses that came through the scan cleanly; never modern translations), credited on the licences page; used by the Poetry page theme and pre-rolls. Page themes also include Roses and thorns and Flower garden (`Realism4.kt`, with the ocean-floor scene: one light per scene, depth of field as soft glows near the lens, haze with distance, many shaded parts; the aurora in `Realism3.kt` is the reference look).
